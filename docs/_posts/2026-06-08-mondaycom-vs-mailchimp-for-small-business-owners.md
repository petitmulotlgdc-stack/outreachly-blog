# [Monday.com](https://monday.com/?via=outreachly) vs [Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com/?via=outreachly): Which Tool Should Small Business Owners Choose?

If you're running a coaching practice, consulting business, or small service company, you've probably heard both names tossed around. But here's the thing—these tools do completely different jobs, and that's where most small business owners get confused.

The real question isn't which one is better overall. It's which one solves your specific problem right now. Let me break this down so you can make the right choice for your business.

## Understanding What Each Tool Actually Does

**Monday.com** is a project management and workflow automation platform. Think of it as your business command center where you organize tasks, manage clients, track deadlines, and collaborate with your team.

**Mailchimp** is an email marketing platform. It's designed to help you build email lists, create campaigns, automate email sequences, and track how your subscribers engage with your messages.

They're not really competitors—they're complementary tools that serve different purposes in your business. However, many small business owners only have budget for one platform initially, so it's worth understanding what each does best.

## Project Management vs Email Marketing: The Core Difference

Monday.com excels at internal organization. It's perfect for:
- Managing client projects and deliverables
- Tracking team workflows
- Keeping deadlines visible
- Automating repetitive tasks within your business operations

Mailchimp shines at external communication. It's ideal for:
- Building and nurturing your email subscriber list
- Sending promotional campaigns
- Creating automated email sequences (like welcome series or follow-ups)
- Analyzing open rates and click-through rates

For a coach or consultant, here's what this means: Monday.com keeps your internal house in order. Mailchimp keeps you connected to your audience and converts subscribers into clients.

## Pricing and Affordability for Solopreneurs

| Feature | Monday.com | Mailchimp |
|---------|-----------|----------|
| **Free Plan** | Basic project board | Up to 500 contacts, basic automation |
| **Starter Plan** | $99/month | Free (forever option) |
| **Mid-Tier Plan** | $199/month | $20-300/month depending on contacts |
| **Best For** | Team collaboration | Email marketing at any scale |
| **Setup Complexity** | Medium | Easy |

For solopreneurs just starting out, Mailchimp's free plan is genuinely powerful. You can manage up to 500 contacts and send unlimited emails without paying a dime. That's huge for someone building their email list from scratch.

Monday.com's free plan exists, but it's fairly limited. Most coaches and consultants find they need a paid plan quickly to unlock automation and advanced features.

## When You Need Monday.com First

Choose Monday.com if:
- You're juggling multiple client projects simultaneously
- You need team members to see and update project progress
- You want to automate internal workflows (like project approval processes)
- You're spending hours in spreadsheets tracking what's due when
- You need to impress clients with professional project visibility

Real example: A business consultant managing five client engagements simultaneously would benefit enormously from Monday.com. They could create a board for each client, assign tasks to team members, set automated reminders, and give clients real-time updates on their project status.

## When You Need Mailchimp First

Choose Mailchimp if:
- You're building an email list and need a simple tool to manage it
- You want to send regular newsletters or updates to subscribers
- You need automated email sequences (welcome series, abandoned cart follow-ups)
- You're testing email marketing before investing heavily in paid platforms
- You want to understand basic email marketing metrics without complexity

Real example: A life coach launching their business would use Mailchimp to capture email addresses from their website, send weekly coaching tips to subscribers, and nurture those relationships until they're ready to buy a program or service.

## The Smart Integration Approach

Here's what many successful small business owners do: start with Mailchimp to build your email marketing foundation (it's free to begin), then add Monday.com once you're managing multiple projects or team members.

They also integrate well together. Your Monday.com workflows can trigger Mailchimp email sequences. When a new client project starts in Monday.com, you could automatically add them to a specific Mailchimp segment for project updates.

## Which Tool Wins for Different Business Models?

**For Solo Coaches:** Mailchimp first (to build your list), then Monday.com if you take on contract team members.

**For Consultants with Teams:** Monday.com takes priority for internal organization.

**For Course Creators:** Mailchimp becomes essential for nurturing your audience and promoting launches.

**For Service Providers:** Both are valuable, but Mailchimp for connecting with prospects, Monday.com for delivering services smoothly.

## Final Recommendation

Start with **Mailchimp** if you're building a business focused on customer relationships and list growth. The free plan is genuinely capable, and you can scale affordably.

Choose **Monday.com** if you're managing complex projects or coordinating with team members. It's worth the investment for operational clarity.

Ideally? Use both. Start with the free Mailchimp plan to test email marketing. Once you're profitable and managing multiple projects or team members, invest in Monday.com. Together, they cover your external marketing and internal operations—the two pillars of a thriving small business.
