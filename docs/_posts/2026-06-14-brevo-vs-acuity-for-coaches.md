# [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/?via=outreachly) vs Acuity for Coaches: Which Platform Wins?

Finding the right email marketing and scheduling platform for your coaching business can feel overwhelming. You need something that handles client appointments, nurtures your email list, and doesn't require a tech degree to set up. The question isn't just about features—it's about which tool actually fits your coaching business model.

Both Brevo and Acuity Scheduling attract coaches for good reasons. But they approach your business differently, and choosing wrong could waste time and money. Let's break down what each platform does best so you can make a confident decision.

## Understanding the Core Purpose of Each Platform

**Brevo** is primarily an email marketing and automation platform that expanded to include SMS and landing pages. It's built for businesses that prioritize email lists, campaigns, and customer communication at scale.

**Acuity Scheduling** is appointment scheduling software designed specifically for service-based professionals like coaches. It handles client bookings, payments, and follow-ups in one system.

Here's the critical difference: Acuity helps you book clients and manage their journey through your services. Brevo helps you stay in touch with prospects and existing clients through email. Many coaches actually use both, but that's not always necessary.

## Pricing: What You'll Actually Spend

**Brevo** offers a free plan with up to 300 email contacts and unlimited emails. Paid plans start around $20/month and scale with subscriber count, making it budget-friendly for growing lists.

**Acuity Scheduling** also has a free plan with basic features, but meaningful capabilities like custom forms and calendar syncing require their paid plan starting around $15/month. Advanced features and higher appointment volumes push costs to $99+/month.

For coaches just starting out, Brevo's free plan genuinely helps you build an email list before paying anything. Acuity's free tier works, but you'll quickly outgrow it if you're booking regular clients.

## Email Automation and Marketing Capabilities

This is where Brevo dominates for coaches building their business.

Brevo's automation is sophisticated. You can create welcome series for new subscribers, nurture sequences for leads, and re-engagement campaigns for inactive contacts. The automation builder is intuitive enough that you won't need a designer.

Acuity includes email capabilities, but they're minimal—mainly transactional emails confirming bookings and sending reminders. If you want to nurture a waitlist or send monthly newsletters, Brevo is far superior.

**Practical example:** You're launching a group coaching program. With Brevo, you build an automated sequence welcoming email subscribers, sharing case studies, and making a time-limited offer. Acuity can't do this effectively.

## Scheduling and Client Management

Acuity is purpose-built for this, and it shows.

Client scheduling includes automated reminders (reducing no-shows), payment collection, and customizable intake forms. Your clients book directly on your calendar, and everything syncs across platforms. Rescheduling is seamless.

Brevo recently added very basic calendar functionality, but it's not comparable to Acuity's appointment management. If scheduling is your primary need, Acuity is the clear winner.

**Practical example:** You coach clients one-on-one with recurring sessions. Acuity handles everything—recurring bookings, automatic reminders 24 hours before, and sending invoices. Brevo would leave you managing this manually.

## Integration and Automation Between Platforms

Most coaches need both email marketing and scheduling.

**Acuity integrates well with:** Zapier, email platforms, and other tools, but you'll need Zapier to connect Acuity to most email systems.

**Brevo integrates with:** WordPress, Shopify, Zapier, and many CRM tools natively. Brevo's ecosystem is more connected if you're building a broader business.

The real question: Do you want two separate platforms talking to each other, or one powerful system handling both?

## Which Platform Should You Choose?

**Choose Brevo if you:**
- Build your business through email lists and content
- Run group programs or courses
- Want sophisticated automation without paying enterprise prices
- Prefer one platform handling most business communications
- Are budget-conscious in the early stages

**Choose Acuity if you:**
- Rely on one-on-one or small group bookings
- Want appointment management built specifically for that purpose
- Value automated reminders reducing no-shows
- Charge per session and need integrated payments
- Prioritize scheduling over email marketing

**The honest answer:** Many successful coaches use Brevo for their email marketing and list building, then layer Acuity on top when appointment volume justifies it. Start with whoever solves your immediate problem, then add the other when you need it.

What's your primary bottleneck right now—managing client appointments or building an engaged email list? The answer tells you where to start.
