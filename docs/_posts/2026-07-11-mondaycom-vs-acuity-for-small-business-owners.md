# [Monday.com](https://monday.com/?via=outreachly) vs Acuity for Small Business Owners: Which Tool Wins?

Managing a small business means juggling countless moving parts. Between client scheduling, project management, payments, and follow-ups, it's easy to feel overwhelmed. You need a tool that keeps everything organized without requiring an MBA to operate.

If you've been researching solutions, you've probably stumbled across Monday.com and Acuity Scheduling. Both are powerful platforms trusted by thousands of coaches, consultants, and entrepreneurs. But which one actually fits your needs? Let's break down the real differences so you can make a confident decision.

## What Each Tool Does Best

**Monday.com** is primarily a work management and project management platform. It helps you visualize workflows, manage team projects, and track progress across departments. Think of it as your command center for organizing tasks and team collaboration.

**Acuity Scheduling**, on the other hand, is built specifically for service-based businesses. It's designed as an all-in-one scheduling, client management, and payment processing solution. If your business revolves around client appointments and consultations, Acuity speaks your language.

## Core Features Comparison

| Feature | Monday.com | Acuity Scheduling |
|---------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Client Scheduling** | Limited | Excellent |
| **Appointment Reminders** | No | Yes (SMS & Email) |
| **Payment Processing** | No | Yes |
| **Project Management** | Excellent | Limited |
| **Automation** | Good | Excellent |
| **Team Collaboration** | Strong | Moderate |
| **Learning Curve** | Moderate | Low |
| **Starting Price** | $99/month | Free tier available |

## Scheduling and Client Management

For coaches and consultants, this is often the deciding factor.

**Acuity Scheduling** was built for service providers. It handles appointment bookings beautifully. Your clients can see your availability in real-time and book directly on your website. You get automatic confirmation emails, SMS reminders (reducing no-shows), and calendar synchronization across all your devices. Payment collection happens seamlessly at booking or after the service.

**Monday.com** has basic calendar features, but it's not its strength. You can create simple scheduling workflows, but it lacks the specialized features service providers need—like client-facing booking pages or automatic reminders that actually reduce cancellations.

## Project and Workflow Management

If you're managing multiple projects or have a team, this changes the equation.

**Monday.com** excels here. Its visual boards, flexible workflows, and team collaboration features make managing complex projects intuitive. You can customize almost anything. Many consultants use it to track client projects, deliverables, and deadlines with their entire team visible on the same page.

**Acuity Scheduling** is lighter on project management. While it tracks client information and service history, it's not designed for managing deliverables across multiple complex projects.

## Automation Capabilities

Both platforms offer automation, but in different contexts.

**Acuity** shines with appointment-related automation. You can create workflows triggered by bookings, cancellations, or no-shows. Automatic follow-up emails, form submissions, and payment reminders save you hours every week. If you're running a coaching practice with 20+ clients monthly, these automations become invaluable.

**Monday.com** provides workflow automation for task management and notifications. You might automate status updates, deadline reminders, or team assignments. It's powerful for operational workflows but doesn't touch the client-service automations Acuity offers.

## Pricing and Setup Time

**Acuity** offers a free tier that works genuinely well for solo practitioners. Their paid plans start at $15/month. Most solo coaches use their Standard plan ($33/month) without hesitation. Setup takes under an hour.

**Monday.com** starts at $99/month with their free tier being quite limited. Setup takes longer because you're building your workflows from scratch. You'll likely spend 4-6 hours configuring it to your needs.

## Who Should Choose Which Tool

**Choose Acuity if you:**
- Offer services requiring client appointments
- Want to collect payments directly
- Work solo or with a small team
- Value quick setup and ease of use
- Need appointment reminders to reduce no-shows

**Choose Monday.com if you:**
- Manage multiple complex projects
- Work with a larger team
- Need visual workflow management
- Have projects beyond just client appointments
- Want extensive customization options

## The Hybrid Approach

Here's a strategy many successful consultants use: Acuity for client scheduling and payments, combined with Monday.com for project management and team collaboration. They integrate reasonably well, and each handles what it does best. You pay more overall, but you eliminate the frustration of forcing a tool to do something it wasn't designed for.

## Final Recommendation

For most solo coaches and consultants starting out, **Acuity Scheduling** is the clear winner. It's purpose-built for your business model, requires minimal setup, includes essential features like payment processing, and costs less than Monday.com.

However, if you're running an agency with multiple projects and team members—or you're a consultant managing complex deliverables alongside client appointments—consider starting with Acuity for client management and adding Monday.com for project oversight.

The best tool is the one you'll actually use consistently. Since Acuity requires less setup and immediately solves your biggest scheduling pain, it deserves serious consideration as your first step toward business organization.
