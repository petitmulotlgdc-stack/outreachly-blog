# [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/?via=outreachly) vs [Hunter.io](https://hunter.io/?via=outreachly) for Consultants: Which Email Tool Wins for Your Practice?

If you're a consultant trying to build your client pipeline, you've probably hit the same wall: finding the right contact information feels like searching for needles in haystacks. You know who you want to reach—decision-makers at specific companies—but getting their actual email addresses takes forever.

This is where tools like Brevo and Hunter.io step in to save the day. But here's the problem: they do very different things, and choosing the wrong one could waste your money and time.

In this guide, I'll break down exactly how these tools work, who should use them, and which one (or both) makes sense for your consulting business.

## Understanding What Each Tool Actually Does

**Hunter.io** is an email finding tool. You give it a domain name, and it hunts through the internet to find email addresses associated with that company. It's laser-focused on one job: helping you locate the people you want to contact.

**Brevo** is an email marketing platform with a much broader toolkit. Yes, it has email finding capabilities through integrations, but it's really designed for building email campaigns, automating follow-ups, managing customer relationships, and tracking results.

Think of it this way: Hunter.io is your prospecting scout. Brevo is your entire marketing operations center.

## Email Finding and List Building

This is where Hunter.io shines for consultants.

Hunter.io's core strength is accuracy and simplicity. You paste in a domain, and within seconds, you get email addresses. You can also upload a list of companies and bulk-find contacts. The tool claims 95% accuracy, and from most user reviews, that holds up pretty well. The pricing is straightforward: you pay based on email searches and API calls.

Brevo offers email finding through partnerships and integrations, but it's not as direct. You're typically limited in how many searches you can do, and the interface isn't as streamlined for pure prospecting work.

**For consultants doing cold outreach:** Hunter.io wins this round. If finding prospects' emails is 80% of your workflow, Hunter.io is your tool.

## Email Campaign Management

Here's where Brevo takes the lead.

Once you have your email list (whether from Hunter.io or anywhere else), Brevo lets you:

- Build automated email sequences
- Set up trigger-based campaigns (someone visits your website, they get an email)
- Create beautiful email templates without coding
- Track opens, clicks, and conversions
- Segment your audience based on behavior
- A/B test subject lines and content

Hunter.io doesn't do any of this. It's purely a finding tool. Once you export your list, you're on your own.

Brevo's automation features are especially valuable for consultants because they let you nurture leads without manually sending each email. If you're juggling multiple client projects, automation becomes your second assistant.

## Pricing Comparison

| Feature | Hunter.io | Brevo |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| **Email Finding** | $50-500/month depending on usage | Free-$300/month (limited) |
| **Email Campaigns** | Not available | Included at all tiers |
| **Automation** | Not available | Included at all tiers |
| **CRM Features** | Not available | Included at Pro+ tiers |
| **Best For** | Finding contacts | Full marketing automation |

Brevo's free plan gives you 300 emails per day, which covers many small consulting practices. Hunter.io has a free plan too, but it's extremely limited (just a few searches).

If you're budget-conscious, Brevo gives you more functionality for less money. If you're doing heavy prospecting, you'll need to pay for both tools.

## Integration and Workflow

For most consultants, the ideal setup is using Hunter.io to find emails, then moving those contacts into Brevo for campaigns and automation.

Both tools integrate with common CRM platforms and email clients, but they integrate differently. Hunter.io has browser extensions that work in LinkedIn and Gmail, letting you find emails directly without leaving those platforms. This speeds up your research significantly.

Brevo integrates with Zapier, which opens doors to connecting it with dozens of other tools you might already use.

## The Real-World Consultant Scenario

Let's say you're a business strategy consultant targeting mid-market tech companies.

**Your workflow:**
1. You identify 50 target companies
2. You use Hunter.io to find the CFOs and COOs at those companies (5 minutes, $5 in credits)
3. You export the list and upload it to Brevo
4. You create a 5-email campaign introducing your services
5. Brevo automatically sends the sequence over two weeks
6. You track who opens the emails and clicks your proposal link
7. You follow up personally with hot leads

Without both tools, this would take hours. With both, it takes 30 minutes plus the time to write good emails.

## Which Tool Should You Actually Buy?

**Choose Hunter.io alone if:** You're doing occasional prospecting, already have an email marketing tool or platform, and mainly need accurate contact info.

**Choose Brevo alone if:** You want an all-in-one solution and don't need heavy-duty email finding (you can manually research or use limited finding features).

**Choose both if:** You're serious about consultant growth, do regular outreach campaigns, and want to automate your follow-up process.

## The Bottom Line

For most consultants, the answer is **both tools, used together**. Hunter.io excels at the hardest part—finding the right person's email. Brevo excels at the important part—getting them to respond through strategic follow-ups.

The combined investment pays for itself with just one or two new consulting clients. If you can only choose one right now, start with Brevo for its broader capabilities, but plan to add Hunter.io within a few months for better prospecting efficiency.
