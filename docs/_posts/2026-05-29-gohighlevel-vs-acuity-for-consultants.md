# [GoHighLevel](https://www.gohighlevel.com/?via=outreachly) vs Acuity for Consultants: Which Platform Wins in 2024?

If you're running a consulting or coaching business, you've probably spent countless hours juggling client calls, proposals, invoices, and follow-ups across different platforms. The struggle is real—and it's costing you time and money.

The question isn't whether you need better tools. It's which platform will actually save you hours each week while helping you land more clients. Today, we're comparing two popular contenders: GoHighLevel and Acuity Scheduling.

## Understanding Your Needs as a Consultant

Before we dive into features, let's be clear about what matters most to consultants and coaches. You need a platform that can:

- Schedule client meetings without back-and-forth emails
- Send professional proposals and contracts
- Manage payments and invoicing
- Track client communications in one place
- Automate routine tasks

The right choice depends on whether you prioritize appointment scheduling, broader business management, or marketing automation.

## GoHighLevel: The All-in-One Business Platform

GoHighLevel positions itself as a complete business operating system. It's designed specifically for consultants, coaches, agencies, and service-based entrepreneurs who want everything in one dashboard.

**Key Strengths:**

GoHighLevel excels at automation and scalability. You get CRM functionality, email and SMS marketing, appointment scheduling, landing pages, and proposal generation all integrated. If you're managing a growing client base, the platform's ability to automate workflows can genuinely free up 5-10 hours per week.

The marketing side is particularly strong. Built-in email sequences, SMS campaigns, and funnel builders help you attract new clients while the CRM keeps existing clients organized. For consultants looking to systematize their lead generation, this is powerful.

**Considerations:**

The learning curve is steeper than some competitors. With so many features, you'll need to invest time in setup or hire someone to configure it properly. Pricing starts around $97/month but scales based on usage and features you unlock. For solopreneurs just starting out, this might feel like overkill.

## Acuity Scheduling: The Specialist's Choice

Acuity Scheduling, now part of Squarespace, is laser-focused on one thing: making appointment scheduling seamless.

**Key Strengths:**

Acuity shines in simplicity and user experience. The scheduling interface is intuitive—both for you and your clients. Customizable intake forms, automatic confirmations, and reminder systems work beautifully out of the box. If your primary pain point is managing calendar chaos, Acuity solves this elegantly.

Payment processing is straightforward, and the platform integrates well with common business tools. For consultants who already have systems in place for CRM and marketing, Acuity is a clean, affordable addition.

**Considerations:**

Acuity is a specialist tool. It doesn't replace your CRM or email marketing platform. You'll still need other solutions for proposal generation, SMS marketing, or complex automation. Starting at $15/month, it's budget-friendly—but you're buying a scheduling tool, not a full business system.

## Which Platform Is Right for You?

**Choose GoHighLevel if:**
- You're actively building a consulting or coaching business
- You want one integrated platform to run operations
- You need marketing automation alongside scheduling
- You're comfortable investing time in setup
- You plan to grow your team or client base significantly

**Choose Acuity Scheduling if:**
- Your main pain point is calendar management
- You already have CRM and marketing tools in place
- You want simplicity and ease of use
- You're a solo consultant with a steady client base
- Budget is a primary concern

## The Practical Recommendation

For most growing consultants, **GoHighLevel offers better long-term value**. Yes, the setup is more involved, but once configured, it eliminates the need for multiple subscriptions (CRM, email marketing, forms, proposals). You'll spend less time switching between tabs and more time with clients.

However, if you're a solo practitioner who simply needs beautiful scheduling with minimal overhead, Acuity is genuinely the better choice. It does one thing exceptionally well, and it won't waste your money on features you don't need.

**The bottom line:** Evaluate your actual needs. Are you managing growth, or managing simplicity? The answer to that question determines your platform.
