# [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/?via=outreachly) vs [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/?via=outreachly) for Freelancers: Which Email Marketing Platform Wins?

Imagine this: You're a coach with a growing client base, and your email inbox is turning into chaos. You're manually tracking client communications, forgetting follow-ups, and losing potential revenue. You need a solution that doesn't require hiring a full marketing team. 

If this sounds like you, you're not alone. Freelancers, coaches, and consultants constantly search for affordable platforms that can automate their marketing without overwhelming them with complex features. The question isn't just "which tool is better?" but rather "which tool helps me actually use it?"

Today, we're comparing two popular email marketing platforms: Brevo and HubSpot. Both promise to streamline your client communication, but they work differently—and for different budgets. Let's break down what matters to freelancers like you.

## Understanding the Core Differences

Before diving into specific features, it's important to understand the fundamental approach each platform takes.

**Brevo** positions itself as an affordable, all-in-one marketing automation suite designed for small businesses and solopreneurs. It started as an email service provider and expanded into SMS, CRM, and automation.

**HubSpot** is an enterprise-focused platform that has branched into small business offerings. Its free CRM and paid tiers create a pathway as your business grows.

The main difference? Brevo aims to be your affordable starting point. HubSpot aims to be your growth partner—sometimes at a higher cost.

## Pricing: The Freelancer's First Concern

This is usually the deciding factor for solopreneurs and small consultancies.

**Brevo** starts free with up to 300 emails per day to unlimited contacts. Paid plans begin at around $20/month for advanced features. This pricing structure makes Brevo incredibly attractive for bootstrapped freelancers who need to prove ROI before investing heavily.

**HubSpot's** free CRM is generous, but email marketing features are limited without paid tiers. Professional plans start around $50/month, jumping to $800+ for enterprise features. For consultants testing the waters, HubSpot's entry cost can feel steep.

**Winner for budget-conscious freelancers:** Brevo

## Ease of Use: Will You Actually Use It?

A fancy platform collecting dust is worthless. Let's talk practical usability.

**Brevo** has an intuitive interface that feels less overwhelming for first-time users. Creating email sequences, setting up basic automation, and managing contacts takes minutes rather than hours. If you're a solo operator without a marketing background, Brevo won't intimidate you.

**HubSpot** has a steeper learning curve. Its interface is powerful but designed for teams with marketing specialists. Setting up workflows, customizing properties, and managing campaigns requires more decision-making upfront.

That said, HubSpot's extensive documentation and tutorials make learning possible—it just takes more time investment.

**Winner for simplicity:** Brevo

## Features That Matter to Coaches and Consultants

Let's focus on what freelancers actually need: email marketing, basic CRM, and automation.

| Feature | Brevo | HubSpot |
|---------|-------|---------|
| Email Marketing | Excellent | Excellent |
| Contact Management | Good | Excellent |
| Marketing Automation | Good | Excellent |
| SMS Marketing | Included | Paid add-on |
| Landing Pages | Included | Included (Pro+) |
| Templates | 200+ | 300+ |
| Free Plan | Yes, generous | Yes, limited |
| Mobile App | Yes | Yes |
| Customer Support | Chat, Email | Chat, Email, Phone (paid plans) |

**Brevo's strength** is bundling SMS marketing into lower tiers—critical if you want to reach clients via text. Its landing page builder and automation sequences work well for coaches sending nurture campaigns.

**HubSpot's strength** is contact intelligence and reporting. If you need detailed analytics about which emails drive conversions, HubSpot provides superior insights. Its CRM is also more sophisticated for managing long-term client relationships.

## Integration Capabilities

Most freelancers use other tools: scheduling software, payment processors, calendar systems, etc.

**Brevo** integrates with 500+ applications, including Zapier, which opens unlimited possibilities. Most integrations work smoothly, though some third-party connections can feel clunky.

**HubSpot** has 1,000+ integrations and tighter connections with popular tools like Slack, Google Workspace, and [Calendly](https://calendly.com/?via=outreachly). For consultants using multiple systems, HubSpot's ecosystem feels more cohesive.

## Customer Support: When You Need Help

**Brevo** offers email and live chat support. Response times are reasonable, though quality varies by region. Free plan users have more limited access.

**HubSpot** provides phone support on paid plans, with superior documentation and community resources. If you prefer live support, HubSpot wins.

## The Verdict: Which Platform for You?

**Choose Brevo if you:**
- Are bootstrapping and need affordability
- Want to start simple without feature overload
- Need SMS marketing included
- Are comfortable with a slight learning curve for third-party integrations

**Choose HubSpot if you:**
- Plan to scale significantly in the next 12 months
- Need advanced contact management and segmentation
- Want enterprise-level support and reporting
- Already use HubSpot for other functions

**Our recommendation for most freelancers?** Start with Brevo. Its generous free plan lets you test email marketing without financial commitment. As your client base grows and you need sophisticated reporting or workflow automation, you can migrate to HubSpot.

The best tool is the one you'll actually use—and for most coaches, consultants, and freelancers, that's the affordable, straightforward option. Brevo gets you started today without breaking the bank.
