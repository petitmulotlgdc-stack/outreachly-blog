# [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/?via=outreachly) vs [Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com/?via=outreachly) for Freelancers: Which Email Platform Should You Choose?

Choosing the right email marketing platform is one of the smartest decisions you can make as a freelancer, coach, or consultant. Your email list is often your most valuable business asset—it's where you build relationships, nurture leads, and generate consistent income without relying on algorithms or paid ads.

But here's the challenge: there are dozens of email platforms out there, and two names keep coming up for freelancers on a budget: Brevo and Mailchimp. Both promise affordable solutions, but they work quite differently. The wrong choice could mean wasting money on features you don't need or struggling with a platform that doesn't scale with your business.

In this article, I'll break down exactly how these two platforms compare so you can make an informed decision for your specific needs.

## Pricing: Where Your Money Actually Goes

Let's start with what matters most to budget-conscious freelancers—cost.

**Mailchimp** operates on a freemium model. You can send unlimited emails to up to 500 contacts for free, which sounds generous. However, once you need more advanced features like automation, landing pages, or A/B testing, you'll need to upgrade to a paid plan. Their paid plans start around $20/month and go up from there.

**Brevo** takes a different approach. Their free plan includes up to 300 emails per day with unlimited contacts. That means you can have 10,000 contacts but still stay in the free tier as long as you don't exceed the daily sending limit. Their paid plans start at around $20/month, but you're getting more automation and features included at lower price points.

For freelancers just starting out, Brevo's free plan is more generous because you're not penalized for having a large list—only for sending frequency. This is a significant advantage if you're building your audience but not sending daily emails yet.

## Automation Capabilities: Working While You Sleep

Email automation is where modern marketing happens. This is how you nurture leads without manually sending follow-ups.

**Mailchimp** offers automation through workflows and automations features, but these are somewhat limited on free and lower-tier plans. You get basic automation like welcome sequences, but sophisticated multi-step campaigns require higher-paid tiers.

**Brevo** includes more robust automation even on lower-tier plans. You can create complex, multi-step automation sequences without paying premium prices. This is particularly valuable for coaches and consultants who need to nurture prospects through education-based sequences before they're ready to buy.

If automation is core to your business model, Brevo gives you more power at a lower cost.

## User Interface: How Easy Is It Actually?

**Mailchimp** is known for being user-friendly and intuitive. Their interface feels modern and polished, which appeals to freelancers who aren't tech-savvy. Setting up campaigns and automations is relatively straightforward.

**Brevo** has made significant improvements to its interface in recent years, but some users find it slightly less intuitive than Mailchimp. The learning curve is steeper, though not dramatic. If you're comfortable spending an hour or two getting familiar with software, you'll adapt quickly.

If simplicity and quick setup are priorities, Mailchimp has a slight edge.

## Integration Ecosystem: Connecting Your Tools

Both platforms integrate with common business tools, but they have different strengths.

**Mailchimp** integrates well with WordPress, Shopify, and popular page builders. If you're building websites with these platforms, Mailchimp offers solid native integration.

**Brevo** offers extensive integrations including CRM features built-in. This is crucial for consultants and coaches because you can manage both your email list and client relationships in one platform.

## Who Should Choose Each Platform?

**Choose Mailchimp if:**
- You value simplicity and quick setup
- Your email list is under 500 contacts initially
- You primarily use WordPress or Shopify
- You prefer a familiar, established platform

**Choose Brevo if:**
- You want more automation at lower cost tiers
- You're building a large contact list
- You need CRM features for client management
- You plan to add SMS marketing later
- You want more advanced features without premium pricing

## The Bottom Line

For most freelancers, coaches, and consultants, **Brevo offers better value**. The generous free plan, included automation, and built-in CRM make it ideal for growing businesses that need sophisticated tools without breaking the bank.

However, if you prioritize simplicity and plan to stay small or you're already invested in the Mailchimp ecosystem, there's no shame in sticking with it.

The real answer? Test both. Most offers provide free trials or free plans long enough to build a small email list and run a basic automation sequence. After that, you'll know which one feels right for your business.

Your email platform is a long-term decision—choose wisely, and it'll pay dividends for years to come.
