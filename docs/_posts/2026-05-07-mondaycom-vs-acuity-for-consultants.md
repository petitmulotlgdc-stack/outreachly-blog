# [Monday.com](https://monday.com/?via=outreachly) vs Acuity for Consultants: Which Project Management Tool Wins?

Managing client projects, deadlines, and deliverables while keeping your consulting business organized is no small feat. You juggle multiple clients, shifting priorities, and endless communication threads. The right project management tool can be the difference between chaos and streamlined operations.

But here's the challenge: Monday.com and Acuity Scheduling each serve different purposes, and choosing between them depends entirely on your consulting workflow. In this guide, we'll break down both platforms so you can make an informed decision for your practice.

## Understanding Your Consulting Needs First

Before comparing these tools, ask yourself: What's your primary bottleneck?

Are you struggling with **project tracking and team collaboration**? Or is your biggest headache **scheduling appointments and managing client availability**?

This distinction is crucial because Monday.com excels at project management, while Acuity Scheduling specializes in appointment scheduling and client booking. Many consultants actually use both tools together rather than choosing one over the other.

## Monday.com: Built for Project-Heavy Consulting

Monday.com is a comprehensive work operating system designed for teams managing complex workflows, multiple client projects, and coordinating across departments.

**Best for:** Consultants managing multiple concurrent projects with team members, deliverables, and detailed timelines.

### Key Features for Consultants

**Flexible automation:** Monday.com's automated workflows save hours every week. You can automatically assign tasks, send status updates, and move projects through stages without manual intervention.

**Visual project tracking:** Use Gantt charts, Kanban boards, or calendar views to track client projects. See dependencies, identify bottlenecks, and keep projects on schedule at a glance.

**Client collaboration:** Many consultants use Monday.com's portal features to give clients visibility into their projects. Clients can see progress without accessing your entire workspace.

**Time tracking integration:** Connect time tracking apps to monitor billable hours per client project—essential for agencies and billing-based consulting.

## Acuity Scheduling: The Appointment Expert

Acuity Scheduling (now part of Squarespace) focuses specifically on automating the booking experience and client scheduling.

**Best for:** Consultants with frequent one-on-one sessions, retainer clients, and appointment-heavy practices.

### Key Features for Consultants

**Automated booking:** Clients book their own sessions directly into your calendar. Set your availability, buffer times, and blackout dates once, then let the system work.

**Intake forms:** Customize intake questionnaires clients complete before their appointment. This information flows directly into your records, saving prep time.

**Automated reminders:** Reduce no-shows with automatic email and SMS reminders. Clients confirm attendance, and your schedule stays protected.

**Integrated payments:** Collect payments right within Acuity. Ideal for consultants charging per session, retainers, or packages.

## Which Tool Should You Choose?

**Choose Monday.com if you:**
- Manage multiple projects simultaneously for different clients
- Work with a team or contractors
- Need detailed timelines, dependencies, and deliverable tracking
- Bill by project rather than by session
- Want comprehensive collaboration and visibility

**Choose Acuity Scheduling if you:**
- Conduct mostly one-on-one consulting sessions
- Have clients booking directly rather than through proposals
- Need strong client self-service booking capabilities
- Collect payments per session or retainer
- Want to minimize administrative scheduling work

## The Hybrid Approach: Using Both Together

Many successful consulting practices actually combine both tools. Here's why:

Use **Monday.com** for your internal project management—organizing deliverables, coordinating team members, and tracking client timelines. Use **Acuity Scheduling** for client appointments, intake forms, and payment collection.

This creates a clean separation: strategic project work lives in Monday.com while scheduling and payment logistics happen in Acuity. Your clients get a polished booking experience, and your team maintains clear project visibility.

Integration is straightforward—both tools connect to Zapier and other automation platforms, so you can sync calendar events between systems.

## Final Recommendation

**For most consultants, start with the tool matching your immediate pain point.**

If you're overwhelmed by appointment scheduling chaos, Acuity Scheduling delivers quick ROI. If you're juggling multiple projects and struggling to keep your team aligned, Monday.com is your priority.

However, if your consulting practice is scaling and you serve multiple clients with ongoing projects, building both into your stack makes sense. The small investment pays dividends in reduced admin time and improved client communication.

The best tool is the one your team actually uses consistently. Choose based on your genuine workflow bottleneck, and you'll find that clarity in operations naturally follows.
