# [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/?via=outreachly) vs [Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com/?via=outreachly) for Freelancers: Which Email Marketing Platform Should You Choose?

As a coach, consultant, or freelancer, your email list is one of your most valuable assets. But choosing the right email marketing platform can feel overwhelming with so many options available. Two platforms that frequently come up in conversations are Brevo and Mailchimp—both popular choices for small business owners managing limited budgets.

The challenge is real: you need a tool that's easy to use, doesn't break the bank, and actually helps you build relationships with your clients and prospects. The wrong choice could mean wasting time on complicated features you don't need or paying more than necessary for your business size.

In this guide, I'll break down both platforms so you can make an informed decision based on your specific freelance business needs.

## Understanding the Core Differences

Before diving into features, it's important to understand the fundamental differences between these two platforms. Brevo (formerly Sendinblue) is a comprehensive marketing platform that combines email marketing with SMS messaging and CRM capabilities. Mailchimp is primarily an email marketing platform that has expanded to include basic automation and landing pages.

For freelancers and solo consultants, this distinction matters. If you want everything integrated in one place, Brevo might appeal to you. If you prefer a simpler, more focused tool, Mailchimp could be the better fit.

## Pricing Comparison: What Will You Actually Pay?

Pricing is often the deciding factor for freelancers working with limited budgets.

**Mailchimp's Pricing Model:**
- Free plan: Up to 500 contacts with limited features
- As you grow beyond 500 contacts, you pay based on subscriber count
- Starting around $20/month for 1,000 contacts

**Brevo's Pricing Model:**
- Free plan: Unlimited contacts (the major advantage)
- Pay-as-you-go: Charged per email sent
- Starting around 2,000 free emails per month, then approximately $20 for 20,000 emails

For freelancers just starting out or with smaller lists, Brevo's unlimited contact advantage is significant. You can nurture a growing audience without worrying about hitting a contact limit that triggers higher fees.

## Automation and Workflow Capabilities

Both platforms offer automation, but at different levels.

Mailchimp's automation features in the free plan are quite limited. To access more sophisticated workflows like abandoned cart emails or multi-step sequences, you'll need to upgrade to a paid plan. The interface is intuitive, making it great for beginners who want simple automations without complexity.

Brevo provides more robust automation even on the free plan. You can create multi-step email sequences, set up triggers based on subscriber behavior, and integrate SMS into your workflows. For consultants running email nurture sequences, this flexibility is valuable.

If your freelance business relies heavily on email sequences to convert prospects into clients, Brevo's automation capabilities give you more room to grow without immediate additional costs.

## Ease of Use and Learning Curve

Mailchimp has earned its reputation for being incredibly user-friendly. The interface is clean, intuitive, and perfect for someone setting up their first email campaign. New users can create and send a professional email in minutes.

Brevo's interface has improved significantly but still requires slightly more navigation. However, it's still accessible for beginners, especially with their growing library of tutorials and documentation.

If you value simplicity and want to get up and running immediately, Mailchimp has the edge. If you're willing to spend a little extra time learning in exchange for more features, Brevo is manageable.

## Integration and Ecosystem

Both platforms integrate with popular tools freelancers use daily.

Mailchimp connects with WordPress, WooCommerce, Zapier, and many other platforms. Its integrations are well-established and stable.

Brevo also offers solid integrations plus its native CRM and SMS capabilities mean less tool-switching. For consultants who want to manage client communications (email, SMS, and follow-ups) in one place, this is a significant advantage.

## Which Platform Should You Choose?

**Choose Mailchimp if:**
- You're brand new to email marketing and want maximum simplicity
- Your contact list is under 500 people and growing slowly
- You primarily need email marketing without SMS functionality
- You prefer a polished, user-friendly interface

**Choose Brevo if:**
- You're managing a growing contact list and want to avoid hitting subscriber limits
- You want built-in SMS marketing capabilities
- You need more sophisticated automation without paying extra
- You want basic CRM features integrated with your email platform

## Final Recommendation

For most freelancers, coaches, and consultants, **Brevo edges out as the better value choice**. The unlimited contacts on the free plan alone makes it worth using, especially as your business grows. The integrated SMS and automation features provide functionality you'd typically need to pay extra for elsewhere.

However, if you absolutely prioritize simplicity and ease of use, and your contact list is small, Mailchimp remains an excellent choice. Start with a free trial of both, send a test campaign, and see which interface feels more natural to your workflow. Your comfort with the platform matters just as much as its features.
