# [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/?via=outreachly) vs [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/?via=outreachly) for Entrepreneurs: Which Email Marketing Platform is Right for Your Business?

Are you drowning in spreadsheets trying to manage your customer relationships? You're not alone. Growing entrepreneurs and consultants often struggle to find an affordable, easy-to-use platform that doesn't require a PhD to operate.

Two platforms consistently rise to the top: Brevo and HubSpot. But which one actually deserves your money and attention? In this guide, I'll break down the real differences so you can make an informed decision for your coaching, consulting, or service-based business.

## Understanding the Basics: What Each Platform Offers

**Brevo** started as a pure email marketing tool (formerly Sendinblue) and has evolved into a customer relationship management platform with email, SMS, and chat features. It positions itself as the affordable alternative for small businesses and solopreneurs.

**HubSpot** is the heavyweight champion of the CRM world, offering a comprehensive suite that includes email marketing, sales tools, customer service, and content management. It's designed to grow with your business.

The key difference? Brevo focuses on being lightweight and budget-friendly, while HubSpot aims to be your all-in-one business operating system.

## Pricing: Where Your Budget Actually Matters

This is where the conversation gets real for most entrepreneurs.

**Brevo's pricing structure** is refreshingly simple:
- Free plan: Up to 300 contacts, unlimited emails to existing subscribers
- Starter plan: $20/month for unlimited contacts
- Higher tiers scale based on your needs

**HubSpot's pricing structure** is more complex:
- Free CRM with basic features
- Starter plan: Starts around $50/month per tool
- Professional and Enterprise tiers get expensive quickly

For a solo consultant or coach with a list under 5,000 contacts, Brevo's starter plan at $20/month is significantly more affordable. If you need multiple HubSpot tools (email + sales + service), you're looking at $100+ monthly quickly.

**Bottom line:** If you're bootstrapped, Brevo wins on price. If budget isn't a constraint, HubSpot offers more features per dollar spent at enterprise scales.

## Features and Functionality: What You Actually Use Daily

| Feature | Brevo | HubSpot |
|---------|-------|--------|
| Email Marketing | ✅ Excellent | ✅ Excellent |
| SMS Marketing | ✅ Included | ✅ Paid add-on |
| Contact Management | ✅ Good | ✅ Excellent |
| Sales Pipeline Tools | ✅ Basic | ✅ Advanced |
| Automation Workflows | ✅ Good | ✅ Excellent |
| Landing Pages | ✅ Limited | ✅ Full builder |
| Reporting & Analytics | ✅ Good | ✅ Comprehensive |
| Ease of Use | ✅ Simple | ⚠️ Steeper learning curve |

**Brevo excels at** straightforward email marketing and customer communication. If you're a coach who needs to send newsletters, launch email sequences, and manage contacts without complexity, Brevo handles this beautifully. The platform is refreshingly intuitive—new users are productive within hours.

**HubSpot excels at** business-scale operations. If you're tracking leads through multiple sales stages, collaborating with a team, and need detailed reporting, HubSpot's advanced features justify its cost.

For most individual consultants and small coaching practices, Brevo's features are more than sufficient.

## Integrations: How Well They Play With Your Other Tools

Both platforms integrate with popular tools like Zapier, WordPress, and [Calendly](https://calendly.com/?via=outreachly), but there are nuances.

**Brevo's integrations** are solid but fewer in number. You'll connect with the essentials—landing page builders, e-commerce platforms, and webinar tools. If you use Zapier, you can bridge almost any gap.

**HubSpot's integrations** are extensive and often deeper. If you're using specialized coaching or consulting software, HubSpot likely has a native integration. This reduces your reliance on workarounds.

That said, unless you're using niche software, Brevo's integration ecosystem handles most entrepreneur needs.

## Customer Support: Getting Help When You Need It

**Brevo** offers email support on free plans and phone support on paid plans. Response times are generally 24-48 hours. They've improved significantly but aren't known for lightning-fast responses.

**HubSpot** provides 24/7 chat and phone support on paid plans. The support team is responsive and knowledgeable. Free plan users get community support and documentation.

If you prefer hands-on guidance, HubSpot's support advantage is meaningful.

## The Real Question: Which Platform for Your Business?

**Choose Brevo if you:**
- Are bootstrapping and need to minimize expenses
- Focus primarily on email marketing and customer communication
- Want a simple, intuitive interface
- Have a list under 10,000 contacts
- Need SMS marketing without additional costs

**Choose HubSpot if you:**
- Have a growing team and need collaborative tools
- Run a sophisticated sales process with multiple stages
- Want advanced automation and reporting
- Can justify the investment in a comprehensive platform
- Anticipate rapid scaling

## Final Recommendation

For most coaches, consultants, and solo entrepreneurs reading this, **Brevo is the smarter choice**. It solves your core problems—email marketing, contact management, and customer communication—at a price that won't strain your cash flow. The learning curve is gentler, and you'll see ROI faster.

However, if you're already managing a team or have complex sales workflows, HubSpot's sophistication and support might be worth the investment.

Start with Brevo. You can always upgrade later as your business scales. Smart entrepreneurs don't solve tomorrow's problems with today's budget.
