# [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/?via=outreachly) vs [Hunter.io](https://hunter.io/?via=outreachly) for Freelancers: Which Email Tool Fits Your Business?

As a freelancer or small business consultant, your ability to reach the right people directly impacts your bottom line. But here's the problem: finding accurate email addresses and managing campaigns efficiently takes time you don't have. You need a tool that lets you build relationships at scale without breaking the bank.

If you've been researching email solutions, you've probably encountered two names repeatedly: Brevo and Hunter.io. Both promise to solve your outreach challenges, but they work differently. One specializes in finding emails, while the other focuses on campaign management and automation. The question isn't which tool is "better"—it's which one solves *your* specific problem.

Let's break down both platforms so you can make a confident decision for your freelance business.

## Understanding Hunter.io: Your Email Finding Partner

Hunter.io does one thing exceptionally well: finding business email addresses. When you need to reach a specific person at a company but only have their name and domain, Hunter.io delivers.

The platform uses advanced algorithms to search across the web and provide verified email addresses. For freelancers doing cold outreach—whether you're a coach pitching potential clients or a consultant reaching industry decision-makers—this is invaluable.

**Key strengths of Hunter.io:**

- **Accuracy matters**: Hunter.io claims 98% accuracy on their email findings, which is crucial when you're building your reputation through outreach
- **Browser extension**: The Chrome extension makes finding emails frictionless as you research prospects
- **Bulk finder**: Need to find 50 emails at once? Hunter.io's bulk finder saves hours
- **Verification**: Emails are verified before delivery, reducing bounce rates

The main limitation? Hunter.io doesn't manage your campaigns. Once you have the emails, you still need another tool to send sequences, automate follow-ups, and track opens.

## Understanding Brevo: Your Complete Campaign Solution

Brevo (formerly Sendinblue) takes a different approach. It's a complete email marketing and CRM platform designed to manage your entire customer communication strategy.

Rather than finding emails, Brevo helps you build contact lists, create professional campaigns, and automate workflows. It's built for entrepreneurs who need to manage relationships, not just collect contacts.

**Key strengths of Brevo:**

- **All-in-one platform**: Email marketing, SMS, CRM, and automation all under one roof
- **Generous free tier**: You can send 300 emails per day forever on the free plan
- **Workflow automation**: Create automated sequences without technical knowledge
- **Contact segmentation**: Organize your audience by behavior, interest, or demographics
- **Affordable scaling**: Paid plans remain budget-friendly as you grow

The trade-off? Brevo doesn't find emails for you. You need to build your contact list through other means—sign-ups, LinkedIn connections, or other sources.

## Which Tool Should You Actually Use?

Here's the real answer: many successful freelancers use **both tools together**.

**Use Hunter.io if:**
- Cold email outreach is a major part of your business development
- You need to reach specific decision-makers quickly
- Accuracy of email addresses directly impacts your conversion rate
- You're scaling your prospecting beyond LinkedIn

**Use Brevo if:**
- You want to nurture relationships through automated sequences
- You need CRM features to track client interactions
- You're building an email list of interested prospects
- Budget is a primary concern (their free plan is genuinely useful)

**Use both if:**
- You're a serious freelancer doing active business development
- You find emails with Hunter.io, then manage campaigns with Brevo
- You want to separate the "finding" and "relationship management" stages

## A Practical Workflow Example

Imagine you're a business consultant targeting companies in your industry. Here's how both tools work together:

1. Research target companies and contact names
2. Use Hunter.io to find verified email addresses (bulk finder)
3. Import those emails into Brevo
4. Create an automated welcome sequence in Brevo
5. Track opens and clicks to identify hot prospects
6. Follow up personally with engaged contacts

This combination turns cold prospects into warm leads efficiently.

## Final Recommendation

For most freelancers, consultants, and coaches starting out: **begin with Brevo's free plan** to manage campaigns and build your email marketing foundation. It costs nothing and handles 80% of email marketing needs.

As your business grows and cold outreach becomes important, **add Hunter.io** to find qualified prospects. The combination gives you the complete toolkit for sustainable business growth.

The best tool isn't the most feature-rich—it's the one you'll actually use consistently. Start simple, measure what works, and upgrade your toolkit as your business demands it.
