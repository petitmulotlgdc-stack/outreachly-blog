# [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/?via=outreachly) vs [Hunter.io](https://hunter.io/?via=outreachly) for Coaches: Which Email Platform Should You Choose?

If you're a coach or consultant trying to build your email list and reach ideal clients, you've probably heard about Brevo and Hunter.io. Both tools promise to help you find and connect with the right people—but they work quite differently, and choosing the wrong one could waste your time and money.

The challenge is real: you need to find prospect emails, segment your audience, and send campaigns that actually convert. But not all tools are built the same way. Some are email finders, others are email marketing platforms, and some do both. Getting this decision wrong means spending months learning a tool that doesn't fit your coaching business.

Let me break down exactly how Brevo and Hunter.io compare, so you can pick the right tool for your coaching practice today.

## What Each Tool Actually Does

**Hunter.io** is an email finding tool. Its primary strength is helping you discover email addresses for people at specific companies. You search for a person's name and company, and Hunter provides their likely business email. It's like having a detective that finds contact information you can't get elsewhere.

**Brevo** (formerly Sendinblue) is a full-stack email marketing platform. Beyond finding emails, it handles email campaigns, automation, SMS marketing, landing pages, and more. It's designed to be your complete marketing hub once you have your contact list.

Think of Hunter.io as the prospecting tool, and Brevo as your complete marketing system.

## Email Finding Capabilities

If your primary need is finding coach prospect emails, Hunter.io wins here decisively.

Hunter.io specializes in email discovery with features like:
- Domain search (find all emails from a company)
- Person search (verify specific contact emails)
- Email verification to check if addresses are valid
- Chrome extension for quick lookups while browsing

For coaches building a targeted list, you can search for decision-makers in specific companies, verify their emails, and add them directly to your outreach. The verification feature is crucial because sending to bad addresses damages your sender reputation.

Brevo doesn't have email finding tools. While it has some audience building features through forms and landing pages, it assumes you already have prospects' contact information. This is fine if you're building organic email lists, but insufficient if you need to actively prospect.

## Email Marketing and Campaign Features

This is where Brevo shines.

Brevo offers:
- Professional email templates
- Advanced segmentation and automation
- A/B testing for subject lines and content
- SMS marketing (a huge advantage for coaches doing follow-ups)
- Lead scoring to identify hot prospects
- CRM features to track client interactions

For coaches sending regular newsletters or nurture sequences to prospects, Brevo's automation is powerful. You can create workflows that automatically follow up when someone opens your email or clicks a link.

Hunter.io focuses purely on finding and verifying emails. It doesn't include campaign sending capabilities, landing pages, or automation. You'd need to export your found emails and use them elsewhere.

## Pricing and Cost Effectiveness

**Hunter.io** pricing starts at $99/month for their pro plan, which includes unlimited searches and 50 verified email credits monthly. Additional credits cost extra if you're finding many prospects.

**Brevo** has a free plan with basic features, making it accessible for coaches just starting out. Paid plans begin around $20/month and scale based on contact volume and features needed. If you want SMS included, expect to pay more.

For coaches on tight budgets, Brevo's free tier is valuable. You can send up to 300 emails daily with basic automation. Hunter.io's free plan is very limited—only 50 searches monthly—which won't sustain serious prospecting.

**Choose Hunter.io if you:**
- Need to actively prospect and find specific coach prospect emails
- Want to build a targeted list from company databases
- Are willing to export emails and use another tool for sending

**Choose Brevo if you:**
- Already have prospect lists or build organically
- Need email automation and nurture sequences
- Want SMS follow-up capabilities
- Prefer an all-in-one platform
- Value budget-friendly pricing with free tier

## The Smart Approach: Use Both

Many successful coaches use both tools together. Here's how:

1. Use Hunter.io to find targeted prospect emails (2-3 hours weekly)
2. Import those emails into Brevo
3. Create automated nurture sequences in Brevo
4. Track opens, clicks, and conversions in Brevo's CRM

This combination gives you active prospecting plus sophisticated marketing automation—the complete client acquisition system.

## Final Recommendation

**For most coaches, I recommend Brevo as your primary tool**, especially if you're new to email marketing. Its affordability, automation features, and SMS capabilities make it invaluable for coaching businesses. Start with the free plan, build your list organically through your website and content, then upgrade as you grow.

**Add Hunter.io** once you've mastered Brevo and want to accelerate prospect research. This sequencing prevents tool overwhelm and ensures you actually use each platform effectively.

The goal isn't having the most tools—it's having the right tools implemented well. Start with Brevo, master it, then expand your stack strategically.
