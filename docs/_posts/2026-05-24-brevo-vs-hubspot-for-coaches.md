# [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/?via=outreachly) vs [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/?via=outreachly) for Coaches: Which Platform Actually Works for Your Coaching Business

You've built a thriving coaching practice. Your clients see results. But managing your client communications, nurturing leads, and automating follow-ups? That's consuming hours you could spend actually coaching.

You're not alone. Most coaches tell us they're juggling email, scheduling, and client management across multiple platforms—wasting time switching between tools instead of focusing on what they do best: transforming lives.

The good news: a solid email marketing and CRM platform can handle all of this. But here's the challenge: should you choose **Brevo or HubSpot**?

Both platforms promise to streamline your coaching business, but they work very differently. Let's break down which one actually fits how you operate.

## How Brevo and HubSpot Differ at Their Core

Brevo is an affordable, straightforward email marketing and CRM platform designed for small businesses and solopreneurs. It excels at simplicity and pricing flexibility.

HubSpot is an all-in-one growth platform with email, CRM, landing pages, and advanced automation. It's more comprehensive but comes with a steeper learning curve and higher costs.

The fundamental difference: **Brevo keeps things simple and budget-friendly, while HubSpot offers more power and complexity.**

For coaches, this distinction matters enormously.

## Pricing: Where Your Budget Actually Goes

Let's be direct about money. Your coaching margins are real, and tool costs impact your bottom line.

**Brevo's Pricing:**
- Free plan: up to 300 contacts, unlimited emails
- Essential plan: starts at $20/month for unlimited contacts
- Scaling upward is gradual and predictable

**HubSpot's Pricing:**
- Free CRM with limited features
- Starter plan: $45/month for email and CRM
- Professional and Enterprise plans go significantly higher ($800+/month)

For a solo coach or small practice with under 5,000 contacts, Brevo's Essential plan typically costs $20-40/month. HubSpot's comparable feature set runs $45-120/month minimum.

**If you're bootstrapping your coaching business, Brevo wins on price by a significant margin.**

## Email Marketing Capabilities for Client Engagement

Both platforms handle email marketing, but with different strengths.

**Brevo's Email Features:**
- Clean, intuitive editor
- Pre-built templates designed for coaches and consultants
- Decent automation for sequences
- SMS marketing included at all paid tiers
- A/B testing available

**HubSpot's Email Features:**
- More advanced personalization options
- Superior integration with other HubSpot tools
- Sophisticated workflow automation
- Better analytics and reporting
- Seamless tracking of client interactions

For most coaches, Brevo's email tools are more than sufficient. You can set up welcome sequences, nurture campaigns, and client follow-ups without needing advanced features. HubSpot shines if you're running complex, multi-touch campaigns with detailed segmentation.

## CRM and Client Management

Here's where the two platforms really diverge.

**Brevo's CRM:**
- Clean, simple contact records
- Basic pipeline and deal tracking
- Useful for tracking client progress and next steps
- Limited customization options
- Good enough for managing 20-50 active clients

**HubSpot's CRM:**
- Highly customizable contact records
- Advanced deal and pipeline management
- Activity tracking and detailed history
- Custom properties and workflows
- Built for scaling to hundreds of client relationships

For a coach managing 15-30 active clients, Brevo handles everything you need. If you're scaling to 100+ clients or managing a team, HubSpot provides better organization and visibility.

## Automation: Setting and Forgetting

Both platforms automate repetitive tasks—but at different sophistication levels.

Brevo offers solid automation for coaches: automatically send welcome emails when someone signs up, trigger follow-ups after a scheduled call, segment your list based on coaching package purchased. It works well for standard sequences.

HubSpot's automation is more powerful. You can build complex workflows that respond to multiple conditions, integrate with your calendar, and sync with other business tools. It's overkill for most coaches but valuable if you're running a scaled operation.

## Integration with Your Coaching Tools

Brevo integrates with:
- Calendar tools (basic)
- Webinar platforms
- E-commerce tools
- Most popular apps via Zapier

HubSpot integrates with:
- Nearly every business tool available
- Deeper native integrations
- Calendar, scheduling, and video conferencing
- Superior ecosystem connectivity

If you use specialized coaching software, check compatibility before deciding. HubSpot generally plays nicer with more tools, but Brevo handles the essentials through Zapier.

## The Recommendation

**Choose Brevo if:**
- You're a solo coach or small group
- Budget matters significantly
- You want setup within hours, not days
- You need straightforward email and basic CRM

**Choose HubSpot if:**
- You're managing a team
- You plan to scale significantly
- You need advanced automation and reporting
- Your budget accommodates higher costs

For most coaches, **Brevo is the smarter choice**. It handles everything you need at a fraction of HubSpot's cost, with less complexity. Start there. You can always migrate to HubSpot later if your needs grow.

Your coaching expertise is your superpower. Pick the tool that lets you focus on that, not tool administration.
