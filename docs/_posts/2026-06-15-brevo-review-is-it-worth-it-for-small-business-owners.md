# [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/?via=outreachly) Review: Is It Worth It for Small Business Owners?

If you're running a coaching practice, consulting business, or small enterprise, you know how overwhelming email marketing can be. You need to nurture leads, build relationships, and stay top-of-mind with your audience—but traditional marketing platforms often come with enterprise price tags that drain your budget before you've sent your first campaign.

This is where Brevo comes in. As a budget-friendly, all-in-one marketing platform, it's been gaining traction among small business owners. But is it actually worth the investment? Let's dive deep into what Brevo offers and whether it's the right fit for your business.

## What Is Brevo and How Does It Work?

Brevo is an all-in-one customer relationship management and marketing automation platform designed specifically for small and medium-sized businesses. Formerly known as Sendinblue, Brevo combines email marketing, SMS campaigns, live chat, and CRM tools into a single dashboard.

The platform positions itself as an affordable alternative to expensive tools like [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/?via=outreachly) or Marketo. Instead of charging per contact or per feature, Brevo uses a credits-based system that gives you flexibility in how you spend your marketing budget.

## Key Features That Matter for Coaches and Consultants

### Email Marketing and Automation

Brevo's email builder is intuitive and beginner-friendly. You can create professional-looking campaigns without coding knowledge, and the drag-and-drop editor makes it simple to customize templates. The automation features are particularly valuable—you can set up workflows that nurture leads automatically based on their behavior.

For consultants and coaches, this means you can create welcome sequences, nurture potential clients through your sales funnel, and follow up with past customers without lifting a finger after the initial setup.

### SMS and WhatsApp Marketing

What sets Brevo apart is its SMS and WhatsApp integration. These channels often have higher open rates than email, making them perfect for time-sensitive offers or appointment reminders. If you're coaching clients or consulting with businesses, SMS campaigns can drive real engagement.

### CRM Capabilities

Brevo includes a basic CRM that helps you track customer interactions and manage relationships. For solo entrepreneurs and small teams, this eliminates the need to purchase a separate CRM platform, saving you money and reducing tool clutter.

### Live Chat

The built-in live chat feature lets you engage website visitors in real-time. For coaches offering discovery calls or consultants answering quick questions, this can improve conversion rates without additional software costs.

## Pricing: Where Brevo Shines

Brevo's pricing model is one of its biggest advantages. The platform offers:

- **Free plan**: Up to 300 emails per day with limited features
- **Starter plan**: Based on credits (flexible usage)
- **Higher tiers**: Progressively more features and higher email volume

For small business owners just starting out, the free plan is genuinely useful. You can send unlimited emails to unlimited contacts—but with a daily sending limit. Many solo entrepreneurs max out this plan for months before needing to upgrade.

When you do upgrade, you're paying for what you use rather than being locked into high monthly fees. This credit-based system appeals directly to businesses with unpredictable marketing needs.

## The Honest Limitations

While Brevo offers impressive value, it's not perfect for everyone.

**Learning curve**: Although the interface is user-friendly, some advanced automation workflows require experimentation. The knowledge base and tutorials could be more comprehensive.

**Customer support**: While available, support isn't as robust as premium competitors. Response times vary, and complex technical issues sometimes take longer to resolve.

**Limited integrations**: Brevo integrates with popular tools like Zapier, WordPress, and Shopify, but it's not as extensive as HubSpot's ecosystem. If you rely heavily on niche software, this could be frustrating.

**Design limitations**: Email templates are functional but less sophisticated than some competitors. If brand aesthetics are crucial, you might need to invest time in customization.

## Is Brevo Worth It for Your Business?

**Brevo is worth it if you:**
- Want an all-in-one platform without enterprise pricing
- Need SMS and email marketing together
- Plan to use basic CRM features
- Have a growing but still small contact list
- Want flexibility in your marketing spend

**Consider alternatives if you:**
- Require advanced automation capabilities
- Need premium customer support
- Depend on extensive third-party integrations
- Have a large team requiring robust collaboration tools

## Final Recommendation

For coaches, consultants, and small business owners, Brevo delivers excellent value. It eliminates the need to juggle multiple platforms while keeping costs reasonable. The SMS and WhatsApp features give you competitive advantages over email-only competitors, and the credit-based pricing means you're never overpaying for unused features.

Start with the free plan to test it out—there's no risk. If it works for your workflow, the upgrade cost is minimal compared to alternatives. For most small businesses at the early growth stage, Brevo is absolutely worth it.
