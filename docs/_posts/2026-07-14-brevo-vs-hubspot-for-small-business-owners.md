# [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/?via=outreachly) vs [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/?via=outreachly): Which Email Marketing Platform Works Best for Small Business Owners?

You've finally grown your coaching or consulting business to the point where email marketing matters. But now you're facing a decision that keeps you up at night: which platform should you choose? Should you go with Brevo, the affordable newcomer, or HubSpot, the industry giant everyone seems to recommend?

The truth is, both platforms can transform how you connect with clients. But they're built for different business needs, budgets, and ambitions. Let me walk you through the key differences so you can make the right choice for your business.

## Understanding Your Email Marketing Needs

Before diving into feature comparisons, ask yourself these three questions:

1. **What's your email list size right now?** Are you managing 500 contacts or 50,000?
2. **Do you need advanced automation?** Or are you looking for straightforward email sends?
3. **What's your budget?** Are you bootstrapped, or do you have marketing dollars allocated?

Your answers will directly influence which platform makes sense for your situation.

## Brevo: The Budget-Friendly Option

Brevo (formerly Sendinblue) has become incredibly popular with small business owners, and for good reason. It's positioned as an affordable alternative to enterprise-level tools.

**Pricing that makes sense:** Brevo offers a free tier with unlimited contacts and up to 300 emails per day. This is genuinely useful if you're just starting. Paid plans begin at just a few dollars per month, making it accessible for coaches and consultants on tight budgets.

**What you get:** Brevo includes email marketing, SMS marketing, and basic CRM features. Their automation capabilities are solid—you can create email sequences, set up behavioral triggers, and segment your audience without paying premium prices.

**The catch:** While Brevo is feature-rich, the interface feels less polished than HubSpot. Reporting and analytics are functional but not beautifully designed. Customer support is adequate but can be slow during peak times.

**Best for:** Solo entrepreneurs, coaches with smaller lists, and consultants who want multiple marketing channels (email + SMS) without breaking the bank.

## HubSpot: The All-in-One Platform

HubSpot is practically a household name in marketing and sales. It's the Swiss Army knife of business platforms, and it shows.

**The comprehensive approach:** HubSpot combines email marketing, CRM, landing pages, forms, and analytics in one seamless ecosystem. Everything integrates beautifully, which saves hours of manual work.

**Pricing structure:** HubSpot's free tier is powerful and includes basic email marketing, a simple CRM, and landing pages. Paid plans start around $50-100/month, which is significantly higher than Brevo.

**Superior user experience:** HubSpot's interface is intuitive and well-designed. Their customer support is exceptional. Learning to use HubSpot feels easier than most platforms, even though it's complex.

**Scalability:** As your business grows, HubSpot grows with you. The platform handles 100,000+ contacts without breaking a sweat, and the advanced automation workflows rival any enterprise tool.

**Best for:** Growing consulting firms, agencies, and coaches building a full marketing and sales system who can invest in premium tools.

## Making Your Decision

**Choose Brevo if:** You're cost-conscious, want SMS marketing included, and your primary need is sending emails to a growing list. You're also managing multiple marketing channels and want them under one roof without premium pricing.

**Choose HubSpot if:** You're building a comprehensive business system that ties together marketing, sales, and customer service. You value user experience, premium support, and don't mind paying more for a platform that scales with your ambitions.

## The Real Talk

Here's what most people won't tell you: both platforms work. Your success with email marketing depends more on your strategy, consistency, and email list quality than on which tool you choose. A coach with great messaging on Brevo will outperform a coach with weak messaging on HubSpot every single time.

That said, if you're managing multiple client touchpoints or planning to build a sophisticated sales process, HubSpot's integration benefits will save you countless hours and prevent frustration down the road.

## Final Recommendation

**Start with Brevo if you have less than 10,000 contacts and want to minimize costs.** The free tier lets you test email marketing without risk, and the paid plans remain affordable as you grow.

**Invest in HubSpot if you're ready to systematize your business and integrate email with your full sales process.** The extra investment pays for itself through time saved on manual tasks and improved customer experience.

The best email platform is the one you'll actually use consistently. Test both with a small campaign, and see which interface you enjoy working with. Your gut feeling often matters more than feature lists.
