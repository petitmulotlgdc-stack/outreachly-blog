# [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/?via=outreachly) vs [Hunter.io](https://hunter.io/?via=outreachly): Which Email Tool Should Freelancers Choose?

Finding the right email tool can make or break your freelance business. Between managing client relationships, following up on leads, and maintaining your reputation, you need software that actually works—not one that adds to your overwhelm.

If you're torn between Brevo and Hunter.io, you're probably wondering which platform will help you build stronger client connections and grow your business faster. Both tools promise to revolutionize how you handle email outreach, but they approach the problem differently.

Let's dig into what each platform actually offers and help you decide which fits your freelance business best.

## Understanding What Each Tool Does

**Brevo** is an all-in-one marketing automation platform designed to handle email campaigns, SMS marketing, and basic CRM features. If you want to manage everything from one dashboard, Brevo positions itself as the Swiss Army knife of email marketing.

**Hunter.io**, on the other hand, is email intelligence software specifically built for finding verified email addresses. It helps you discover and verify contact information for people you want to reach—perfect if you're cold prospecting or researching potential clients.

The key difference: Brevo is your email *sending* platform, while Hunter.io is your email *finding* tool. Many successful freelancers actually use both together in their workflow.

## Email Finding and Verification Capabilities

This is where Hunter.io truly shines. If you're targeting specific companies or industries, Hunter.io's database contains over 100 million verified professional email addresses.

Here's what makes Hunter.io powerful for freelancers:

- **Domain search**: Enter a company URL and instantly see all verified email addresses from that organization
- **Email verification**: Before sending campaigns, confirm that addresses are valid
- **Accuracy rates**: Hunter.io boasts 95%+ accuracy on their verified emails, meaning fewer bounces

Brevo offers basic email validation but doesn't specialize in *finding* new contacts. You'll need to build your list another way or import existing contacts.

For coaches and consultants doing cold outreach, this matters significantly. You don't want to waste time manually researching contacts or sending emails that bounce.

## Email Campaign and Automation Features

Brevo transforms once you have a contact list. The platform excels at:

- **Email marketing campaigns**: Create professional templates, schedule sends, and track opens/clicks
- **Automation workflows**: Set up triggered sequences based on subscriber behavior
- **Segmentation**: Target specific groups with personalized messaging
- **SMS marketing**: Reach clients through another channel
- **CRM basics**: Store client information and track interactions

Hunter.io doesn't offer email campaign functionality. It's purely a research and verification tool.

If you're running regular newsletters, nurture sequences, or multi-touch outreach campaigns, Brevo is essential. Hunter.io just gets you the right email addresses to send to.

## Pricing Comparison

| Feature | Brevo | Hunter.io |
|---------|-------|-----------|
| **Free Plan** | Up to 300 emails/day, 500 contacts | 50 searches/month, limited verification |
| **Starter Plan** | ~$20/month | ~$99/month |
| **Best For** | Budget-conscious freelancers | Active prospectors |
| **Primary Cost Driver** | Email volume | Email searches and verification |

Brevo's free plan is genuinely generous for freelancers just starting out. You can send daily campaigns and manage automation without paying anything.

Hunter.io's free tier gets you started, but if you're actively prospecting, you'll hit limits quickly.

## Ease of Use and Learning Curve

**Brevo** has a typical marketing automation interface—it takes some learning, but the platform provides good tutorials. Freelancers with email marketing experience will feel at home. Beginners might need 2-3 hours to feel comfortable.

**Hunter.io** is refreshingly simple. Type a domain name, review results, verify emails, and export. Most people understand it within minutes.

Neither tool has a steep learning curve, but they're designed for different workflows.

## Which Tool Should You Actually Use?

Here's the honest answer: **it depends on your business model**.

**Choose Hunter.io if you:**
- Are actively cold prospecting for new clients
- Want to reach specific companies or decision-makers
- Need verified email addresses for outreach accuracy
- Are willing to pay for quality contact data

**Choose Brevo if you:**
- Already have a client or contact list
- Want to send regular newsletters and campaigns
- Need email automation and workflows
- Are budget-conscious and just starting out

**The best approach?** Use both together. Many successful freelancers use Hunter.io to find prospects, then import those contacts into Brevo for automated follow-up sequences. This two-tool strategy maximizes your outreach effectiveness while keeping costs reasonable.

If you can only choose one, start with **Brevo**. Its generous free plan lets you build email marketing skills without investment, and you can find contacts manually or through other free tools until your outreach generates real revenue.

Once you're landing consistent clients and ready to scale your prospecting, add Hunter.io to your toolkit.

Your freelance business will thank you when you're spending less time searching for email addresses and more time building real relationships with clients.
