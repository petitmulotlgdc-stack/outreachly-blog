# [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/?via=outreachly) vs Acuity for Small Business Owners: Which Email & Scheduling Platform Is Right for You?

If you're juggling client bookings, email campaigns, and follow-ups all at once, you know how quickly things can spiral out of control. For coaches, consultants, and small business owners, having the right tools to manage customer relationships and automate workflows isn't just nice to have—it's essential to scaling your business without burning out.

Two platforms consistently pop up in conversations about client management: Brevo and Acuity. But which one actually delivers what you need? Let's break it down so you can make an informed decision for your business.

## Understanding the Core Differences

Brevo (formerly Sendinblue) has evolved from a straightforward email marketing tool into a more comprehensive business platform. It excels at email automation, SMS marketing, and contact management with a heavy emphasis on affordability.

Acuity, owned by Squarespace, takes a different approach by combining scheduling, client management, and payment processing into one integrated system. It's built specifically with service-based businesses in mind—think coaches, therapists, consultants, and photographers.

The key difference? Brevo is email-first with add-ons, while Acuity is client-relationship-first with robust scheduling at its core.

## Email Marketing & Automation Capabilities

**Brevo's Email Strengths:**
Brevo offers unlimited contacts on most plans, which is a game-changer for growing businesses. You get access to drag-and-drop email builders, pre-built templates, and solid automation workflows. The platform also includes SMS marketing, which Acuity doesn't offer natively. If email volume is your priority, Brevo is extremely cost-effective.

**Acuity's Email Features:**
Acuity includes email functionality but positions it as a supporting tool rather than the star attraction. You can send emails and set up automated sequences, but the builder and template library aren't as extensive as Brevo's. However, emails integrate seamlessly with your client profiles, appointment history, and payment records.

For email-focused campaigns, Brevo wins. For integrated client communication tied to appointments and payments, Acuity integrates better with your overall workflow.

## Scheduling & Appointment Management

This is where Acuity truly shines. 

Acuity was built with scheduling at its heart. Clients can book appointments directly on your website or branded booking page, with automated confirmation emails, reminders, and follow-ups. You can set service packages, pricing tiers, and complex availability rules. If a client needs to reschedule, they can do it with one click.

Brevo doesn't have native appointment scheduling. You'd need to integrate it with a third-party tool like [Calendly](https://calendly.com/?via=outreachly) or use their API to build something custom.

If you're spending time manually scheduling clients back-and-forth via email, Acuity eliminates that headache immediately.

## Pricing Structure & Overall Value

| Feature | Brevo | Acuity |
|---------|-------|--------|
| Entry-Level Price | Free (500 contacts) | $15/month |
| Email Marketing | Excellent | Basic |
| SMS Marketing | Included | Not included |
| Scheduling | Not included | Core feature |
| Payment Processing | Not included | Included |
| Automation Workflows | Good | Very good |
| Ease of Setup | Easy | Moderate |
| Best For | Email volume | Client services |

**Brevo's Pricing:** The free plan includes up to 500 contacts and 300 daily emails. Paid plans start around $20/month for unlimited contacts and more features. You only pay based on email volume or monthly spend—no per-contact fees above a certain threshold.

**Acuity's Pricing:** Starts at $15/month for basic features and scales to $99/month for advanced functionality. Every plan includes appointment scheduling, payments, and client management. If you need SMS, you'd still need a separate tool.

For pure email marketing, Brevo is cheaper. For all-in-one client management with scheduling, Acuity's pricing is competitive.

## Integration & Customization

Both platforms integrate with popular tools like Zapier, allowing you to connect them to your CRM, payment processors, or other business software.

Brevo excels in flexibility—you can use it as a standalone email tool or build complex automation with integrations. Acuity provides tighter integrations within Squarespace's ecosystem but stays more focused on core scheduling and client management.

If you're building a custom tech stack, Brevo offers more flexibility. If you want everything working seamlessly together out of the box, Acuity's integrated approach is simpler.

## Making Your Decision

**Choose Brevo if you:**
- Send high volumes of emails regularly
- Need SMS marketing capabilities
- Want the most affordable entry point
- Plan to integrate with multiple tools
- Run a lead-generation or content-heavy business

**Choose Acuity if you:**
- Manage client appointments and bookings
- Need to collect payments from clients
- Want a streamlined, all-in-one solution
- Offer services with scheduled time slots
- Prefer simplicity over extensive customization

## The Recommendation

For most coaches, consultants, and service-based small business owners, **Acuity is the better choice**. The appointment scheduling and payment processing eliminate daily friction, and the integrated client management keeps everything organized. You're saving time on admin work that could be spent with clients.

However, if you're primarily focused on email marketing and already have a booking system in place, **Brevo offers exceptional value** and flexibility at a lower price point.

The sweet spot? Some businesses use both—Acuity for scheduling and payments, Brevo for email campaigns and SMS marketing—leveraging each platform's strengths without overlap.

Start with a free trial of both and see which workflow feels more natural to your business. The right tool should feel invisible, letting you focus on what you do best: serving your clients.
