# [Apollo](https://www.apollo.io/?via=outreachly) vs [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/?via=outreachly) for Consultants: Which CRM Really Works Better?

You've finally decided it's time to upgrade your consultant business with a proper CRM system. But now you're stuck between two popular options: Apollo and HubSpot. Both promise to streamline your sales process, but which one actually delivers the results you need?

The truth is, choosing the wrong CRM can cost you months of productivity and thousands of dollars in wasted features. As a consultant, you need a tool that helps you find prospects, manage relationships, and close deals—without unnecessary complexity.

Let's break down how Apollo and HubSpot stack up against each other, so you can make a decision that actually fits your business.

## What Each Platform Does Best

**Apollo** is a sales engagement and prospecting platform built specifically for teams that need to find and contact leads quickly. It includes a database of over 275 million business profiles, making it excellent for outbound prospecting.

**HubSpot** is a broader CRM platform that covers sales, marketing, customer service, and operations all in one place. It's designed to be a complete business system rather than just a prospecting tool.

This fundamental difference shapes everything else about how they work.

## Prospecting and Lead Generation

One of the biggest advantages Apollo offers is its built-in prospecting engine. With access to a massive B2B database, you can search for decision-makers by job title, company size, industry, and location. For consultants who do outbound business development, this is invaluable.

Apollo also includes email finding, phone number verification, and automated email sequences. You can create campaigns and track engagement right within the platform.

HubSpot's lead generation capabilities are more limited out of the box. While you can import lists and create smart segments, HubSpot doesn't include a prospect database. You'll need to either build your own list or integrate with third-party prospecting tools. This adds extra steps to your workflow if you're actively sourcing new clients.

## Ease of Use and Setup

HubSpot has a reputation for being user-friendly, and that's partially true. The interface is intuitive, and getting started is straightforward. However, once you dig into advanced features, there's a learning curve—especially if you're using multiple modules.

Apollo has a steeper initial learning curve. The interface is packed with features, and there are more buttons and options to navigate. However, if your primary goal is prospecting and sales engagement, you'll get up to speed quickly because everything is focused on that function.

For consultants who want minimal setup friction, HubSpot wins. For those willing to invest a bit of time to master a specialized tool, Apollo gets you results faster.

## Pricing Comparison

This is where the decision often gets made.

**Apollo** starts at $49 per month for the basic plan, which includes database access and limited sequences. Professional plans run $165-$299 per month, depending on features and users.

**HubSpot** starts with a free tier, but that's quite limited. Their Starter plan is $50 per month, Professional is $800 per month, and Enterprise is $3,200 per month. The pricing jump between tiers is significant.

For solo consultants and small teams, Apollo is substantially more affordable while still being powerful for prospecting. HubSpot's pricing accelerates quickly if you need multiple modules or seats.

## Which Tool Is Right for Your Consulting Business?

**Choose Apollo if:**
- You're focused on outbound prospecting and business development
- You want to build a strong pipeline of potential clients
- You need affordable, specialized prospecting tools
- Your team is small (1-3 people)
- You prefer a tool that does one thing exceptionally well

**Choose HubSpot if:**
- You want a complete CRM that handles sales, marketing, and customer service
- You need detailed customer journey tracking and reporting
- You're planning to scale your business operations across multiple departments
- You want seamless integration with marketing automation
- You prefer an all-in-one platform, even if it costs more

## Final Recommendation

For most consultants, **Apollo is the smarter choice**. Here's why: consultants thrive on quality relationships and consistent client acquisition. Apollo's prospecting database and email engagement tools directly address the biggest pain point consultants face—finding and connecting with ideal clients.

The cost savings also matter. At $49-$299 per month, you can afford Apollo while it's generating revenue. HubSpot's pricing becomes a burden until your business is significantly larger.

That said, if you're building a larger consulting firm with multiple team members handling different functions—business development, customer success, and operations—HubSpot's integrated approach eventually makes sense.

Start with Apollo to build your pipeline and prove your business model. Once you're scaling and need sophisticated multi-department workflows, you can migrate to HubSpot. Your future self will thank you for the cost savings in the meantime.
