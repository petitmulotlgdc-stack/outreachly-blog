# [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/?via=outreachly) Review: Is It Worth It for Consultants?

If you're running a consulting business, you know that staying connected with your clients is everything. Yet managing emails, automation, and client communications can quickly become overwhelming—especially without the right tools. Many consultants find themselves juggling multiple platforms, paying steep subscription fees, and still not getting the results they need.

That's where Brevo comes in. But is this all-in-one email and CRM platform actually worth the investment for consultants like you? Let's dive into what Brevo offers and whether it delivers real value for your business.

## What Is Brevo and How Does It Work?

Brevo is an affordable email marketing and CRM platform designed for small businesses, consultants, and entrepreneurs. Previously known as Sendinblue, the platform rebrand reflects its evolution into a more comprehensive business communication tool.

The platform combines email marketing, SMS campaigns, chat functionality, and basic CRM features into one dashboard. Unlike enterprise solutions that require extensive setup and training, Brevo positions itself as accessible to business owners who want automation without complexity.

When you log in, you'll find features for building contact lists, creating email campaigns, automating sequences, and tracking client interactions. The interface is relatively intuitive, making it possible to get started within hours rather than days.

## Pricing: Is It Actually Affordable?

One of Brevo's biggest selling points is its pricing structure. Here's what makes it attractive for consultants:

**Free Plan**: You get up to 300 emails per day with unlimited contacts. This works well if you're just starting or have a small client base.

**Paid Plans**: Starting around $20-$40 per month for basic automation and CRM features. This is significantly cheaper than competitors like [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/?via=outreachly) or [Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com/?via=outreachly)'s premium tiers.

**What You Get**: Email templates, contact segmentation, automation workflows, and basic CRM functionality at these price points.

However, advanced features like landing pages and advanced reporting require higher-tier plans. For most consultants managing 50-500 active clients, the mid-tier plans offer solid value without breaking the budget.

## Core Features That Matter for Consultants

### Email Automation
Brevo shines when it comes to email automation sequences. You can create welcome series, follow-up campaigns, and nurture sequences without manual work. This is crucial for consultants who want to maintain relationships without spending hours on emails.

### Contact Management and Segmentation
The CRM component lets you organize clients by service type, engagement level, or custom fields. This segmentation capability means you can send targeted messages to specific groups—say, all clients enrolled in your Q1 program—without affecting others.

### SMS Marketing
Beyond email, Brevo offers SMS campaigns. For consultants, this means you can send appointment reminders or quick updates via text, which often has higher open rates than email.

### Integration Capabilities
Brevo integrates with popular tools like Zapier, allowing you to connect it with your calendar, payment processors, or other business software. However, some consultants find the native integrations more limited than competitors.

## Real Consultant Use Cases

**Scenario 1**: A business coach managing 100+ active clients uses Brevo to send monthly newsletters and automated follow-up sequences after consultations. The cost savings versus HubSpot allows her to invest more in content creation.

**Scenario 2**: An IT consultant uses SMS reminders for quarterly check-ins and email automation to nurture leads who visit his website. The basic CRM tracking helps him remember client preferences between engagements.

**Scenario 3**: A marketing consultant finds that Brevo's contact segmentation lets her run separate campaigns for retainer clients versus one-off project clients, improving relevance and response rates.

## Potential Drawbacks to Consider

While Brevo offers strong value, it's not perfect for everyone:

- **Limited landing page builder**: Unlike some competitors, creating conversion-optimized landing pages requires more manual work
- **CRM is basic**: If you need sophisticated pipeline management, you might outgrow Brevo
- **Support**: Free plan users have limited support access, which can be frustrating
- **Learning curve**: While generally user-friendly, some automation setup requires experimentation

## Is Brevo Worth It for You?

**Choose Brevo if you**:
- Want affordable email marketing with CRM features
- Need solid automation without enterprise complexity
- Manage a small to mid-sized client base (under 1000 contacts)
- Value SMS marketing capabilities
- Have a tight marketing budget

**Look elsewhere if you**:
- Need advanced CRM pipeline management
- Require extensive integrations with specialized tools
- Want premium landing page builders
- Prefer 24/7 phone support

## Final Recommendation

For most consultants, Brevo delivers excellent value. It's affordable, functional, and easier to learn than enterprise platforms. The combination of email, SMS, and basic CRM in one tool eliminates the need for multiple subscriptions.

Start with the free plan to test it out. If you find yourself regularly using the features, upgrade to a paid plan. You'll likely spend less than $50 monthly and gain tools that significantly improve client communication and nurturing.

The real question isn't whether Brevo is worth it—it's whether you can afford not to have email automation and CRM in your consulting business. For that purpose, Brevo is one of your best options at this price point.
