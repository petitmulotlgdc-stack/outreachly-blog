# [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/?via=outreachly) vs Acuity for Small Business Owners: Which Email Marketing Platform Should You Choose?

If you're running a coaching, consulting, or small business, you've probably wondered which email marketing platform would actually save you time and money without overwhelming you with unnecessary features. The choice between Brevo and Acuity Scheduling is more important than you might think—it directly impacts how you communicate with clients, automate your workflows, and ultimately, grow your business.

The problem is that most platforms force you to choose between affordability and functionality. You end up either paying too much for features you'll never use, or settling for a basic tool that leaves you frustrated when you need more advanced automation. Let's dig into how Brevo and Acuity compare so you can make the right decision for your business.

## Understanding Your Email Marketing Needs First

Before comparing these platforms, consider what matters most to your business. Are you primarily focused on email campaigns and list building? Do you need advanced scheduling and automation? Are you running a service-based business that needs appointment booking? Your answers determine which platform will actually serve your growth.

Coaches and consultants typically need robust email automation, easy list segmentation, and integration with their favorite tools. Let's see how these two platforms stack up.

## Brevo: Affordability Meets Functionality

Brevo is an all-in-one marketing platform that's gained serious traction with small business owners who want professional features without enterprise pricing.

**What Brevo does well:**

Brevo's biggest strength is its generous free plan. You get up to 300 emails per day with unlimited contacts—that's a game-changer for bootstrapped businesses. The interface is intuitive, and you'll get up and running in minutes, not days.

The platform includes email marketing, SMS campaigns, CRM, and marketing automation in one place. This means fewer tool subscriptions and less time switching between platforms. For coaches and consultants, this integrated approach simplifies client communication.

Brevo's automation features are robust. You can create sophisticated sequences based on user behavior, purchase history, or engagement levels. The template library is extensive, and even without design skills, you'll create professional-looking emails.

Pricing scales affordably as you grow. Even at higher contact volumes, Brevo remains cost-effective compared to competitors.

## Acuity Scheduling: The Specialist Approach

Acuity Scheduling, now part of Squarespace, specializes in appointment booking and client management. It's the tool many service-based businesses reach for first.

**What Acuity excels at:**

If your primary need is scheduling appointments and managing client calendars, Acuity is phenomenal. The booking system is seamless—clients book directly from your calendar without back-and-forth emails. Acuity handles reminders, confirmations, and follow-ups automatically.

The client management features are solid. You track client history, manage intake forms, and handle payments all within the platform. For consultants managing multiple clients, this centralization is valuable.

However, Acuity's email marketing capabilities are more limited. While it handles transactional emails and reminders well, it's not designed for sophisticated email campaigns or advanced segmentation. If email marketing is central to your strategy, Acuity falls short here.

## Which Platform Matches Your Business?

**Choose Brevo if you:**
- Run a coaching or consulting business focused on list-building and campaigns
- Need sophisticated email automation and segmentation
- Want to minimize monthly tool costs
- Need SMS marketing capabilities
- Prefer an all-in-one platform to reduce subscription bloat

**Choose Acuity if you:**
- Primarily need calendar and appointment management
- Want client intake forms and automated confirmations
- Require integrated payment processing for bookings
- Have minimal email marketing needs beyond transactional messages

## The Hybrid Approach

Many successful consultants and coaches actually use both platforms together. They use Acuity for appointment scheduling and client management, then connect Brevo for their email marketing campaigns and advanced automation. This combination gives you the best of both worlds—specialized tools doing what they do best.

## Final Recommendation

For most coaches, consultants, and small business owners, **Brevo is the better standalone choice**. It offers greater flexibility, more powerful email marketing features, and significantly lower costs. The generous free plan lets you test it with zero financial commitment.

However, if appointment scheduling is your primary pain point and you're willing to use a separate email platform for campaigns, Acuity Scheduling excels at that specific job.

Your best move? Start with Brevo's free plan. Test it for 30 days. If you need advanced scheduling capabilities later, you can always add Acuity to your stack. This approach minimizes risk while maximizing your business tools' effectiveness.
