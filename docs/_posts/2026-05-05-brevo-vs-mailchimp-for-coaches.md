# [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/?via=outreachly) vs [Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com/?via=outreachly) for Coaches: Which Email Platform Actually Works Better?

Your email list is your most valuable business asset. But choosing the right platform to manage it? That's where coaches and consultants often get stuck.

You've probably heard both Brevo and Mailchimp mentioned in Facebook groups, podcasts, and coaching communities. They're everywhere. But they're not the same—and picking the wrong one could mean leaving money on the table or overpaying for features you'll never use.

Let me be direct: the choice between Brevo and Mailchimp depends entirely on your coaching business model, budget, and automation needs. I'm going to walk you through both so you can make an informed decision.

## Understanding Your Email Marketing Needs as a Coach

Before we compare these platforms, let's talk about what actually matters for your coaching business.

As a coach, you need:
- A reliable way to nurture leads between sessions
- Automated follow-up sequences for webinar registrants
- Simple segmentation to send relevant content to different client groups
- Affordable pricing that scales with your business

Both Brevo and Mailchimp offer email marketing. But they're built differently, and those differences matter.

## Brevo vs Mailchimp: Head-to-Head Comparison

| Feature | Brevo | Mailchimp |
|---------|-------|----------|
| Free tier | Up to 300 contacts, unlimited emails | Up to 500 contacts, limited features |
| Automation workflows | Included in all plans | Available from standard plan ($20/month) |
| SMS marketing | Included (pay per send) | Paid add-on ($20/month minimum) |
| CRM features | Built-in CRM included | Limited, requires paid add-on |
| Email templates | 300+ ready-made templates | 100+ templates |
| Customer support | Chat and email support | Email support only on free plan |
| Pricing model | Contacts-based | Contacts-based (with limitations) |
| Ease of use | Good, steeper learning curve | Very intuitive for beginners |

## Brevo: The All-in-One Platform Choice

Brevo positions itself as an all-in-one marketing platform. If you want email, SMS, CRM, and landing pages in one place, this is appealing.

**Where Brevo shines for coaches:**

Automation is built into every plan, even the free tier. This means you can create welcome sequences, lead nurturing campaigns, and follow-ups without paying extra. If you run webinars or challenges, this is huge.

The included CRM is valuable. You can track client interactions, note follow-ups, and see your entire customer relationship in one platform. For coaches managing multiple clients or leads, this reduces context-switching.

SMS marketing is baked in. You can combine email and SMS in single automation workflows. Need to send a reminder text before a coaching session? Done without hunting for integrations.

**The downsides:**

The interface takes time to learn. New users often find the dashboard overwhelming compared to Mailchimp. It's powerful, but you might spend your first week clicking around.

Pricing is contact-based, starting at 20,000 contacts. That's actually quite generous, but the jump from their higher tiers gets expensive fast compared to Mailchimp.

## Mailchimp: The Beginner-Friendly Platform

Mailchimp dominates the "easiest to use" category, and that's not by accident. They've spent years perfecting the user experience.

**Where Mailchimp wins for coaches:**

It's genuinely intuitive. If you've never used email marketing software, you'll feel comfortable within minutes. The onboarding is clean, and the interface doesn't overwhelm you with options.

The free tier is generous for small coaching practices—500 contacts is real breathing room if you're just starting.

Standard automation is simple and effective. You don't get advanced sequences without upgrading, but basic "welcome email after signup" automation works smoothly.

Integration marketplace is excellent. Need to connect your coaching platform, payment processor, or scheduling tool? Mailchimp plays nicely with most popular services.

**The limitations:**

Automation costs extra ($20/month minimum), which adds up when you're bootstrapping a coaching business.

The free tier has restrictions. You'll hit limits on automations, landing pages, and segmentation faster than with Brevo.

No native SMS or CRM. Adding these features means additional tools and additional subscriptions.

## Practical Recommendation for Coaches

Here's my honest take:

**Choose Brevo if:**
- You want email, SMS, and CRM all in one platform
- You're planning to scale quickly and need advanced automation
- You don't mind spending time learning a more complex interface
- You want to avoid juggling multiple subscriptions

**Choose Mailchimp if:**
- You're just starting and want the easiest onboarding possible
- Your coaching business primarily needs email (not SMS or CRM features)
- You want a platform that "just works" without learning curves
- You plan to integrate with specialized coaching or payment tools

## The Bottom Line

Both platforms work for coaches. Brevo offers more features and better value if you use them. Mailchimp offers simplicity and beginner-friendly design.

If you're a solo coach with one or two offerings, start with Mailchimp. You'll get up and running in an afternoon, and the free tier is genuinely useful.

If you're running a scaling coaching business with multiple programs, clients, and lead generation efforts, Brevo's all-in-one approach and included automation will serve you better long-term.

Either way, you can't go wrong. Pick one, set it up, and focus on what actually grows your coaching business—delivering results for your clients.
