# [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/?via=outreachly) vs [Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com/?via=outreachly) for Small Business Owners: Which Email Platform Wins?

If you're running a coaching practice, consulting business, or small enterprise, you've probably wrestled with this question: which email marketing platform should you actually use?

Your email list is one of your most valuable business assets. It's where you build relationships, nurture leads, and generate consistent revenue without relying on social media algorithms. But choosing the wrong email platform can waste your time, money, and frustration.

The two platforms dominating conversations right now are Brevo and Mailchimp. Both promise to help you reach your audience, but they work very differently. Let's cut through the marketing language and give you the practical comparison you need to make the right choice for your business.

## Understanding the Core Differences

Mailchimp has been around since 2001, and it's the platform most small business owners know first. It's familiar, it's free (with limitations), and millions of businesses use it.

Brevo (formerly Sendinblue) is the newer competitor gaining serious traction. It was built from the ground up as an all-in-one platform combining email, SMS, and marketing automation—not as an afterthought bolted onto a basic email tool.

This fundamental difference shapes everything about how each platform works and who should use them.

## Pricing: The Real Cost Comparison

**Mailchimp's pricing model** is deceptively simple: it's free for up to 500 contacts with unlimited emails. Sounds amazing, right? The catch: their free plan includes their branding in your emails, limited automation, and basic features only.

Once you grow beyond 500 contacts or need professional features, you'll pay:
- $20/month for 1,000 contacts
- $350/month for 10,000 contacts
- Pricing scales with your list size

**Brevo's approach** is different. You pay based on how many emails you send, not how many contacts you have. Their free plan includes:
- Up to 300 emails per day (unlimited contacts)
- Full automation features
- SMS capabilities
- Their branding in emails

If you send more, plans start at $20/month for 20,000 emails monthly. This matters because you can have 50,000 contacts and only pay if you're actively emailing them.

**For small business owners**, Brevo often wins on pricing once you're serious about your business. You get more features at lower costs, especially if you have a large list but don't email everyone every day.

## Automation Capabilities

As a coach or consultant, automation is everything. It's the difference between working 60 hours a week and building a business that actually runs itself.

**Mailchimp's automation** has improved significantly, but it's still somewhat limited. You get:
- Basic email sequences
- Simple conditional logic
- Tag-based automation
- Limited integration options

For simple nurture sequences and welcome series, it works fine. For complex, multi-step campaigns with multiple decision points? You'll quickly hit its ceiling.

**Brevo's automation** is built for sophisticated marketing. You get:
- Advanced workflow builder with unlimited steps
- Complex conditional logic
- Journey-based automation
- SMS + email automation combined
- Integration with hundreds of tools

If you're running a coaching business with multiple onboarding paths, different client tiers, or complex sales funnels, Brevo's automation actually saves you tremendous time.

## Deliverability and Reputation

Here's something people don't talk about enough: if your emails don't land in the inbox, nothing else matters.

Mailchimp's reputation has taken hits in recent years. Some users report consistently landing in spam folders, especially with cold email campaigns or larger sends.

Brevo has built a reputation for excellent deliverability. Their infrastructure is newer and more sophisticated. If inbox placement is critical to your business (and it should be), this matters.

## The User Experience Factor

Mailchimp is intuitive if you're just starting. Click around, you'll probably figure it out.

Brevo has a steeper learning curve initially, but once you understand the system, it's incredibly powerful. The interface can feel overwhelming if you only need basic email sending, but elegant if you need sophisticated marketing.

## Which Should You Choose?

**Choose Mailchimp if:**
- You're brand new to email marketing and want simplicity
- You have a small list (under 1,000 contacts)
- You send occasional emails without complex automation
- Budget is your absolute primary concern upfront

**Choose Brevo if:**
- You're serious about building an automated business
- You need SMS marketing alongside email
- You want advanced automation and sophisticated campaigns
- You're concerned about email deliverability
- You plan to grow and want a platform that scales with you

For most coaches, consultants, and small business owners reading this—the ones who want to build real businesses, not just dabble—**Brevo is the better choice**. You get professional features, better automation, SMS capabilities, and pricing that rewards growth rather than punishes it.

Start with Brevo's free plan. Build your email sequences, test the automation, and see if it fits your workflow. You'll either love the power it gives you, or you'll quickly understand why simplicity matters for your particular business.

The right email platform is the one that actually gets used and drives results. Make your choice based on your actual business needs, not just feature lists.
