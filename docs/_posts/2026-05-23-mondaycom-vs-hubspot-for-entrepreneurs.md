# [Monday.com](https://monday.com/?via=outreachly) vs [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/?via=outreachly) for Entrepreneurs: Which Tool Wins for Your Business?

Running a coaching, consulting, or small business means juggling countless moving parts. Client relationships, project timelines, sales pipelines, and team coordination all demand attention. Without the right system, you're left switching between tabs, missing deadlines, and losing track of important follow-ups.

If you're looking to streamline your operations, you've probably heard about Monday.com and HubSpot. Both platforms promise to transform how you work, but they approach business management differently. The question isn't which is "better"—it's which one fits your specific needs and budget.

Let's break down these two powerhouses so you can make an informed decision.

## What Each Platform Does Best

**Monday.com** is a work operating system designed for flexibility. It helps you manage projects, track workflows, and collaborate with your team through a highly customizable dashboard. Think of it as the Swiss Army knife of project management.

**HubSpot** is a customer relationship management (CRM) platform built around sales, marketing, and customer service. It's designed to help you manage leads, nurture relationships, and automate communications. It's the specialist in relationship management.

The key difference? Monday.com excels at *how you work*, while HubSpot excels at *managing your customers and sales*.

| Feature | Monday.com | HubSpot |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| **Project Management** | Excellent | Basic |
| **CRM Functionality** | Limited | Excellent |

| **Team Collaboration** | Strong | Moderate |
| **Sales Pipeline Tracking** | Available | Primary focus |

## Monday.com: Best for Project-Focused Entrepreneurs

If you manage multiple client projects, coordinate team deliverables, or run service-based businesses, Monday.com shines.

**Why coaches and consultants love it:**
- Visual workflow management with customizable boards
- Easy to track client project progress
- Strong team collaboration features
- Flexible enough to adapt to any business process
- Great automation for repetitive tasks

For a coach managing multiple client programs, you might create a board for each client with stages like "Intake," "Active," "Completion," and "Follow-up." You can attach resources, set deadlines, and keep everything visible at a glance.

**The catch:** Monday.com isn't built for sales pipeline management or customer relationship tracking. If lead nurturing and sales automation are critical, you'll need additional tools.

## HubSpot: Best for Sales-Driven Entrepreneurs

HubSpot's strength lies in managing customer relationships and automating your sales process from prospect to client.

**Why entrepreneurs choose HubSpot:**
- Built-in CRM with lead tracking and scoring
- Powerful email marketing automation
- Contact management with detailed history
- Sales pipeline management with forecasting
- Free tier available to get started
- Excellent reporting and analytics

If you're a consultant acquiring new clients regularly, HubSpot helps you track every interaction, automate follow-ups, and see your pipeline at a glance. The platform practically guides leads toward becoming clients.

**The downside:** HubSpot has a steeper learning curve, and you'll quickly outgrow the free version. Project management isn't its forte, so if you're juggling complex deliverables, you'll need another tool.

## Pricing Comparison

**Monday.com** starts at $99/month for a single user, with higher tiers at $199/month and $499/month. You'll pay per user, which adds up quickly for larger teams.

**HubSpot** offers a free CRM tier, making it accessible for bootstrapped entrepreneurs. Paid plans start at $50/month for the Sales Hub and increase from there depending on features and team size.

If budget is tight, HubSpot's free option is unbeatable. If you need advanced project management, Monday.com is worth the investment.

## The Real Question: Which Solves Your Pain?

Ask yourself these questions:

- **Are you struggling to track and manage client projects?** → Monday.com
- **Are you losing leads or struggling with sales follow-up?** → HubSpot
- **Do you need both project management and CRM?** → Consider using both (they integrate)

Many successful entrepreneurs use *both platforms together*. Monday.com handles internal operations and project delivery, while HubSpot manages the front-end sales and customer relationships. This combination costs more but eliminates the need to compromise on either function.

## Our Recommendation

**For coaches and consultants primarily focused on project delivery to existing clients:** Choose Monday.com. Its flexibility and collaboration features make it ideal for managing coaching programs, client deliverables, and team coordination.

**For entrepreneurs focused on growth and lead conversion:** Choose HubSpot. The free tier lets you start immediately, and the CRM automation will accelerate your sales process.

**For ambitious entrepreneurs managing both:** Use both. The integration works smoothly, and you'll have a complete system covering client acquisition through project delivery.

Your tool choice should reflect your biggest bottleneck. Address that first, then add complementary tools as you scale. Neither Monday.com nor HubSpot will fix a broken business process—but whichever you choose, you'll have a solid foundation to build on.
