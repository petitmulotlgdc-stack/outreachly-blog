# [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/?via=outreachly) vs [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/?via=outreachly) for Consultants: Which Platform Should You Choose?

You've built a thriving consulting practice, but managing client communications, email campaigns, and follow-ups is eating into your billable hours. Sound familiar?

The good news: the right email marketing and CRM platform can automate these tasks and free up your time. The challenge? Deciding between popular options like Brevo and HubSpot, which offer similar features but work quite differently for consultants.

This guide compares Brevo and HubSpot to help you choose the platform that actually fits your business—and your budget.

## Understanding Your Consulting Needs

Before comparing specific features, let's clarify what consultants typically need from a CRM and email platform:

- **Email automation** for nurturing leads and staying in touch with past clients
- **Contact management** to organize client information and interaction history
- **Basic reporting** to track campaign performance
- **Integration capabilities** to connect with tools you already use
- **Affordable pricing** that scales with your business

Both Brevo and HubSpot address these needs, but they approach them differently.

## Pricing: The Most Obvious Difference

If budget is your primary concern, this section matters most.

**Brevo** wins the affordability round. Their free plan includes unlimited contacts and email sending up to 300 emails per day—perfect for consultants just starting with automation. Paid plans begin around $20/month and scale based on daily sending volume, not contact count. This means you can have 50,000 contacts and still pay a reasonable fee if you're not sending daily emails.

**HubSpot** takes a different approach. The free CRM is genuinely useful and includes contact management, basic email, and sales pipeline tools. However, the Marketing Hub (where email campaigns live) starts at $50/month. For the full suite of features consultants want, expect to spend $120-300/month.

**Winner for consultants on a budget:** Brevo

## Email Marketing and Automation

Both platforms excel at email, but with different strengths.

**Brevo** offers straightforward email marketing with solid automation workflows. You can create triggered emails based on contact behavior, segment audiences, and build drip campaigns. The interface is clean and intuitive—you won't need weeks of training. For consultants sending regular newsletters or nurture sequences, Brevo delivers everything you need without unnecessary complexity.

**HubSpot** provides more sophisticated automation and deeper personalization options. If you want to build complex multi-step workflows with conditional logic across dozens of criteria, HubSpot handles it elegantly. The platform also excels at account-based marketing if you work with specific target accounts.

**Winner for most consultants:** Brevo (simplicity and results)  
**Winner for complex campaigns:** HubSpot

## Contact Management and CRM Features

Your CRM should help you understand and serve clients better.

**Brevo's** CRM features are functional but basic. You'll organize contacts, track deals, and log interactions. It works—especially if you're transitioning from spreadsheets. However, it lacks some advanced features power users expect.

**HubSpot's** CRM is feature-rich and specifically designed to grow with your business. The free tier is legitimately comprehensive. You get detailed contact records, deal tracking, task management, and communication history all in one place. The mobile app is particularly strong.

**Winner for CRM depth:** HubSpot  
**Winner for CRM simplicity:** Brevo

## Integration and Ecosystem

Your consulting tools need to work together.

**Brevo** integrates with common business tools: Zapier (giving you access to 7,000+ apps), WordPress, WooCommerce, and popular CRM systems. If you use standard tools, you're covered.

**HubSpot** has native integrations with hundreds of platforms, including Google Workspace, Slack, Salesforce, and most productivity tools consultants use. The integration ecosystem is more polished and requires less configuration.

**Winner for integration ease:** HubSpot

## Which Platform Should You Choose?

**Choose Brevo if you:**
- Want to keep costs minimal while scaling
- Prefer simplicity over advanced features
- Send regular newsletters or nurture sequences
- Don't need sophisticated deal tracking
- Work as a solo consultant or small team

**Choose HubSpot if you:**
- Can invest in a comprehensive platform
- Need advanced automation and personalization
- Want a platform designed to scale with growth
- Value excellent integration support
- Manage multiple clients with complex workflows

## Final Recommendation

For most consultants starting with automation, **Brevo is the smarter choice**. You get powerful email marketing, basic CRM features, and affordable pricing that won't burden your budget. The platform does what consultants need without unnecessary complexity.

If you're running a larger consulting firm with multiple team members, complex sales processes, or specific integration needs, **HubSpot's investment pays dividends** through improved efficiency and team collaboration.

Start with Brevo's free plan, test the platform, and expand when you genuinely need advanced features. You can always migrate to HubSpot later—most consultants find Brevo serves them well for years.
