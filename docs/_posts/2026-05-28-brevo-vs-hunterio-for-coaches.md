# [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/?via=outreachly) vs [Hunter.io](https://hunter.io/?via=outreachly): Which Email Tool Should Coaches Choose?

If you're a coach or consultant struggling to build a sustainable client pipeline, your email strategy matters more than ever. But here's the challenge: you need the right tools to find prospects AND keep them engaged once you've made contact.

Two platforms keep coming up in conversations with coaches and consultants: Brevo and Hunter.io. While they're often mentioned together, they actually solve different problems. Understanding which one fits your coaching business is crucial before you invest your time and money.

Let me break down everything you need to know.

## What Brevo Does (And Doesn't)

Brevo is an all-in-one marketing platform that focuses on email campaigns, SMS marketing, and customer relationship management. Think of it as your complete communication hub once you already have contacts in your system.

For coaches, Brevo shines when you need to:
- Send professional email sequences to prospects and clients
- Automate follow-ups after initial contact
- Build email workflows based on client behavior
- Create SMS campaigns for appointment reminders
- Segment your audience based on interests or engagement

The platform offers a free plan that includes up to 300 contacts and basic automation—which is genuinely useful for solo coaches just starting out. Paid plans give you access to more advanced features like advanced segmentation and priority support.

## What Hunter.io Does (And Doesn't)

Hunter.io is an email finding tool. Its primary job is simple: help you discover email addresses for people you want to reach.

If you've identified a prospect company or found someone on LinkedIn, Hunter.io can tell you their likely email address. It works by analyzing patterns and checking domain data to predict business email formats.

For coaches, this matters because:
- You can find contact information for potential clients without cold-calling or sending awkward DMs
- You can verify if an email address is accurate before reaching out
- You can research entire companies to find multiple decision-makers
- You can build prospect lists much faster than manual research

Hunter.io operates on a credit-based system, and even their free plan gives you a reasonable number of searches each month (typically 100 email finder searches).

## Key Differences in Practical Use

**Email Finding vs. Email Sending**

Here's the fundamental difference: Hunter.io helps you *find* the person to email. Brevo helps you *send* the email and track what happens next. These tools don't compete—they complement each other.

**For Building Your Prospect List**

You'll start with Hunter.io. If you want to reach marketing managers at tech companies, Hunter.io helps you locate their actual email addresses quickly. Without this, you're either guessing at email formats or sending messages to generic "info@" addresses that nobody reads.

**For Converting Prospects Into Clients**

Once you have email addresses, Brevo takes over. You can craft welcome sequences, share valuable content, and automate follow-ups while you focus on coaching. Brevo tracks opens and clicks so you know who's actually interested.

## The Ideal Workflow for Coaches

Here's how successful coaches typically use these tools together:

**Step 1:** Use Hunter.io to research and find email addresses for your ideal clients (30 minutes per week)

**Step 2:** Add those email addresses to Brevo as new contacts (manual or via automation)

**Step 3:** Send a personalized first email through Brevo explaining who you are and offering value

**Step 4:** Create a 5-7 email sequence in Brevo that builds trust and demonstrates your coaching methodology

**Step 5:** Track engagement in Brevo and personally reach out to those who open/click

This approach combines the finding power of Hunter.io with the automation and tracking power of Brevo.

## Which Tool Should Coaches Actually Buy?

**Get Hunter.io if:** You spend time researching prospects but struggle with finding actual email addresses. This is the bottleneck for most coaches starting their outreach.

**Get Brevo if:** You already have a contact list or way to find emails, but your follow-up is inconsistent or you're manually sending individual emails.

**Get both if:** You want a sustainable, scalable client acquisition system. Together, they cost less than most coaching-specific software, and they're infinitely more flexible.

## Final Recommendation

For most coaches and consultants, start with **Hunter.io**. The free plan is genuinely useful, and once you've tried it for a few weeks, you'll know if finding accurate email addresses is your real bottleneck.

Then add **Brevo** when you have enough prospects to justify automation. The free plan covers up to 300 contacts—enough for a meaningful email campaign—so you can test before spending money.

This two-tool combination gives you the complete picture: finding the right people to reach AND staying on their radar once you've made contact. That's the foundation of sustainable coaching business growth.
