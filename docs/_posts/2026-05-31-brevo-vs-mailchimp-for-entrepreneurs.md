# [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/?via=outreachly) vs [Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com/?via=outreachly) for Entrepreneurs: Which Email Marketing Platform Should You Choose?

If you're running a coaching business, consulting practice, or small company, your email list is one of your most valuable assets. But choosing the right platform to manage it can feel overwhelming—especially when comparing popular options like Brevo and Mailchimp.

Both platforms promise affordable solutions with powerful features, but they work differently and serve different needs. In this guide, I'll break down the key differences so you can make the right choice for your business.

## Understanding the Basics: What Sets Them Apart

Brevo (formerly Sendinblue) and Mailchimp are both email marketing platforms, but they approach the market differently.

Mailchimp started as a free, simple email marketing tool and has evolved into a broader marketing platform with automation, landing pages, and CRM features. It's widely known and used by millions of small businesses.

Brevo positions itself as a more comprehensive marketing automation platform that includes SMS marketing, transactional emails, and CRM—often at more competitive pricing than Mailchimp.

The core difference? Mailchimp excels at simplicity and accessibility, while Brevo offers more features in its lower pricing tiers.

## Pricing: Which Offers Better Value for Your Budget

This is where things get interesting for entrepreneurs watching their cash flow.

**Mailchimp's pricing structure:**
- Free plan: Up to 500 contacts, limited features
- Paid plans: Start at $20/month for up to 1,000 contacts
- Premium features unlock as you move up pricing tiers
- Significant jumps when you exceed contact limits

**Brevo's pricing structure:**
- Free plan: Unlimited contacts, up to 300 emails/day
- Paid plans: Start at €20/month ($22 USD)
- SMS credits and automation included in most tiers
- More predictable scaling as your list grows

For coaches and consultants building their email list, Brevo's unlimited contacts on the free plan is a game-changer. You can grow your list without worrying about hitting a contact limit that triggers a price jump.

**Automation and Workflows**

If you run a coaching or consulting business, automation saves you countless hours. Brevo includes marketing automation on its free plan, while Mailchimp reserves this for paid users. This matters when you want to automatically send welcome sequences or trigger emails based on customer behavior.

**Segmentation Capabilities**

Both platforms let you segment your list, but Brevo's segmentation is more granular. You can create complex conditions without jumping to a higher pricing tier, which is crucial when personalizing your messages to different client groups.

**CRM and Contact Management**

Brevo has a built-in CRM for managing client relationships. Mailchimp has basic contact management but feels more limited. For consultants managing multiple client relationships, Brevo's integrated approach reduces tool-switching.

## Integration Ecosystem: Connecting Your Business Tools

Your email platform needs to play well with the other tools you use—calendar apps, payment processors, webinar platforms, and CMS tools.

Mailchimp has broader integrations simply because it's been around longer and more developers have built connections. If you use Zapier extensively or rely on niche tools, check integration availability carefully.

Brevo offers strong integration options and has been expanding rapidly. For most entrepreneurs using popular tools (WordPress, Shopify, WooCommerce, payment processors), both platforms work well.

The edge goes to Mailchimp for ecosystem breadth, but Brevo covers most common use cases adequately.

## Ease of Use: Learning Curve and Support

Mailchimp wins on familiarity. It's been the go-to for years, so you'll find countless tutorials, YouTube videos, and community answers online. If you prefer an intuitive, straightforward interface, Mailchimp feels lighter and less overwhelming.

Brevo's interface has improved significantly, but it's still slightly more feature-rich (which means slightly more complex). However, their documentation is solid, and the platform is genuinely easier to navigate than it was a few years ago.

For beginners, Mailchimp has a gentler learning curve. For power users wanting more capabilities without paying premium prices, Brevo becomes more comfortable quickly.

## Making Your Decision: Which Platform Wins for Entrepreneurs?

**Choose Mailchimp if:**
- You want maximum simplicity and minimal learning curve
- You value the largest integration ecosystem
- You don't mind paying for automation features
- You have a small list under 1,000 contacts

**Choose Brevo if:**
- You want marketing automation and CRM included from day one
- You're budget-conscious and expect rapid growth
- You want SMS marketing capabilities
- You prefer unlimited contacts on the free plan

## The Bottom Line

For most coaches, consultants, and entrepreneurs, **Brevo offers superior value**. You get more features at lower price points, automation isn't locked behind paywalls, and the unlimited contacts on the free plan removes artificial growth ceilings.

However, if simplicity and familiarity matter most to you, and you don't need advanced automation immediately, Mailchimp is a solid choice that won't let you down.

Start with a free trial of both, send a test campaign, and see which platform feels right for your workflow. The best platform is the one you'll actually use consistently—and both can grow with your business.
