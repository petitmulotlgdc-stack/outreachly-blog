# [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/?via=outreachly) vs [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/?via=outreachly) for Small Business Owners: Which CRM Actually Delivers?

You're running a lean operation. Every dollar counts. Every minute matters. So when it comes to choosing a CRM and email marketing platform, you need to be strategic about where your investment goes.

The problem? Most small business owners, coaches, and consultants get overwhelmed by feature lists that don't apply to their business. They end up paying for tools that do too much, when they really just need something that works.

If you're torn between Brevo and HubSpot, you're not alone. Both platforms promise to transform your customer relationships and boost your revenue. But which one actually delivers for your specific situation?

Let's break down the real differences so you can make an informed decision.

## Pricing: Where Your Budget Actually Goes

When you're bootstrapping or operating on a tight budget, pricing isn't just a line item—it's a deciding factor.

**Brevo** wins on affordability for pure email marketing and basic CRM needs. You get up to 300 contacts and unlimited emails completely free. Their paid plans start at around $20/month and scale gradually as you grow.

**HubSpot** offers a free tier too, but it's more limited. The free CRM includes basic contact management and deal tracking, but you're practically nudged toward their paid plans almost immediately. HubSpot's entry-level plans start around $50/month, and professional features quickly push you to $800+/month.

For solo coaches and consultants just starting out, Brevo's pricing structure feels more forgiving.

## Email Marketing Capabilities: Core Functionality Comparison

Here's where the platforms show their strengths and weaknesses.

Brevo excels as an email marketing platform first. Their automation workflows are intuitive, their template library is extensive, and A/B testing is built-in across all plans. If email is your primary marketing channel (and for many coaches, it absolutely is), Brevo feels natural and purposeful.

HubSpot treats email as one feature among many in a comprehensive ecosystem. It's solid, but it's not the main event. Their email tools are powerful, but they're designed to work in conjunction with their sales, service, and marketing hubs—which means more features you might not immediately need.

## CRM and Sales Features: Beyond Email

If you're managing client relationships and need robust sales tracking, this section matters.

**Brevo's CRM** is functional but leaner. It handles contact management, deals, and basic pipeline tracking well. For small teams and solo operators managing 20-50 active clients, it's more than adequate. You won't feel overwhelmed by complexity.

**HubSpot's CRM** is more sophisticated. It includes detailed activity tracking, advanced reporting, custom properties, and workflow automation that goes deeper. If you're coordinating between team members or need detailed forecasting, HubSpot's sales hub becomes compelling.

| Feature | Brevo | HubSpot |
|---------|-------|--------|
| **Pricing Entry Point** | Free (300 contacts) | Free (limited features) |
| **Email Marketing** | Best-in-class | Solid but secondary |
| **CRM Sophistication** | Adequate | Advanced |
| **Automation Workflows** | Good | Excellent |
| **Learning Curve** | Easy | Moderate to steep |
| **Best For** | Email-focused businesses | Sales-driven teams |
| **Customer Support** | Good | Excellent |

## Ease of Use: Time Investment Required

You don't have bandwidth to learn complex software.

Brevo's interface is straightforward. Navigating between email campaigns, contacts, and automation feels intuitive. New users can launch their first campaign within 30 minutes. The platform rewards you for getting started quickly rather than penalizing you for not exploring advanced features.

HubSpot has a steeper learning curve. The platform is incredibly powerful, but that power comes with complexity. You'll benefit from their extensive knowledge base and tutorials, but expect to spend more time getting comfortable. Many small business owners find themselves needing a specialist just to configure everything properly.

## Integration Ecosystem: Making Your Tools Talk

Integration matters when you're juggling multiple platforms.

Both Brevo and HubSpot integrate with popular tools like Zapier, WordPress, Shopify, and Stripe. HubSpot's integration marketplace is larger and more polished. If you're building a sophisticated tech stack with many moving parts, HubSpot provides more native integrations.

For most coaches and consultants, though, the difference is negligible. Both platforms play well with standard business tools.

## Support and Community: Help When You Need It

HubSpot offers superior customer support, particularly on paid plans. Their documentation is comprehensive, and their academy provides certification programs.

Brevo's support is responsive but less extensive. For straightforward questions, you'll find answers quickly. For complex customization, you might hit a wall.

## The Final Word: Which Should You Choose?

Choose **Brevo** if:
- Email marketing is your primary revenue driver
- You're managing a small client base (under 100 active contacts)
- Budget is a primary concern
- You want simplicity over complexity
- You're a solo operator or very small team

Choose **HubSpot** if:
- You need advanced sales tracking and forecasting
- You're coordinating across multiple team members
- You're willing to invest time in learning the platform
- You need extensive integrations and customization
- Your budget allows for $50+ monthly investment

For most coaches and consultants starting out, **Brevo represents better value**. It does what you need without forcing you to pay for features you won't use. As you scale and your needs become more sophisticated, migrating to HubSpot becomes a natural next step.

Start with what serves your business today, not what you think you might need tomorrow.
