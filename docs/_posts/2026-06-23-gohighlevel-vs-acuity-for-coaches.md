# [GoHighLevel](https://www.gohighlevel.com/?via=outreachly) vs Acuity for Coaches: Which Platform Should You Choose?

Running a coaching business means juggling countless moving parts—client scheduling, payment processing, marketing, and follow-ups. The difference between a thriving practice and one that's constantly chaotic often comes down to having the right tools in place.

If you're researching coaching business software, you've probably stumbled across both GoHighLevel and Acuity. Both platforms promise to streamline your operations, but they take very different approaches. In this guide, I'll break down what each platform offers, who they're best for, and help you make an informed decision for your specific needs.

## What Is GoHighLevel?

GoHighLevel is an all-in-one business platform designed specifically for coaches, consultants, and service-based professionals. Think of it as your complete operating system for running a coaching business—it includes scheduling, CRM, email marketing, SMS messaging, invoicing, funnels, and more, all in one ecosystem.

The platform emphasizes automation and workflow efficiency. You can create custom client journeys, automate follow-ups, and manage your entire business without switching between multiple tools.

## What Is Acuity?

Acuity (owned by Squarespace) is primarily a scheduling and appointment management platform, though it has expanded to include some business features. Its strength lies in its beautiful, user-friendly interface and seamless scheduling experience.

Acuity focuses on getting clients booked and paid quickly. It's less about creating complex automations and more about having a clean, professional booking system that clients love to use.

## Core Differences in Functionality

### Scheduling and Booking

Both platforms excel at scheduling, but in different ways. Acuity's booking experience is industry-leading—clients find it intuitive, and the interface is visually appealing. GoHighLevel's scheduling is equally functional but requires more customization to achieve the same level of polish.

If your primary concern is having beautiful, frictionless client booking, Acuity wins here. However, GoHighLevel's scheduling integrates seamlessly with its other tools, so when someone books, their information automatically populates in your CRM.

### Marketing and Client Management

This is where the platforms diverge significantly. GoHighLevel includes built-in email sequences, SMS campaigns, landing pages, and webinar tools. You can nurture leads, segment your audience, and automate entire marketing funnels without leaving the platform.

Acuity has basic email capabilities, but it's not designed for sophisticated marketing. If you want to send promotional campaigns, create email sequences, or build sales funnels, you'll need to integrate with external tools like [Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com/?via=outreachly) or Convertkit.

### Automation and Workflows

GoHighLevel's automation engine is powerful. You can create complex workflows: when a client books a session, automatically send them a pre-call questionnaire. When they complete it, send a confirmation email with a video prep guide. Create custom follow-up sequences after each coaching call.

Acuity offers basic automation—appointment reminders and follow-up emails—but nothing approaching GoHighLevel's sophistication.

## Pricing Considerations

Acuity's pricing starts at $15/month for basic features and goes up to $99/month. This affordability makes it appealing for coaches just starting out or those running lean operations.

GoHighLevel's entry point is $99/month, with packages scaling to $499/month for advanced features. While higher, you're getting significantly more functionality—potentially eliminating the need for separate email marketing, SMS, CRM, and funnel-building tools.

## Which Platform Is Right for You?

**Choose Acuity if you:**
- Want a straightforward, beautiful scheduling tool
- Are keeping your tech stack simple
- Prefer affordability and ease of use
- Don't need automated marketing sequences
- Plan to use external tools for marketing and CRM

**Choose GoHighLevel if you:**
- Want an integrated all-in-one platform
- Need automation for client management and marketing
- Want to eliminate tool switching and integration headaches
- Plan to scale your coaching business
- Want built-in funnels and landing pages
- Prefer having everything in one ecosystem for simplified billing

## Final Recommendation

For most growing coaching practices, **GoHighLevel** offers better long-term value. While it has a steeper learning curve and higher price tag, the all-in-one nature means you're not paying for separate email platforms, SMS services, or CRM tools. The automation features alone save coaches hours every week.

However, if you're just starting out, testing your market, or genuinely prefer simplicity over features, **Acuity** is an excellent choice. Pair it with a free or cheap email tool, and you have a functional system that won't overwhelm you.

The best platform is the one you'll actually use consistently. Consider starting a free trial with whichever matches your current priorities, then upgrade as your business grows.
