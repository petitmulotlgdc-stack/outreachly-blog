# [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/?via=outreachly) vs [Hunter.io](https://hunter.io/?via=outreachly): Which Email Tool Should You Choose?

You're building your business. You've got great services. But here's the problem: you can't find the right people to talk to, and even when you do, you're not sure how to reach them effectively.

If you're a coach, consultant, or entrepreneur trying to grow your business through email outreach, you've probably heard about Brevo and Hunter.io. Both promise to help you find email addresses and connect with potential clients. But they work differently, and choosing the wrong one could waste your time and money.

In this guide, I'll break down exactly what each tool does, who they're best for, and how to pick the right one for your business.

## What Is Hunter.io and What Does It Do?

Hunter.io is an email finding tool. Its main job is simple: you give it a company name or website, and it finds email addresses for people who work there.

Here's how it works in practice: You're interested in reaching the marketing manager at a mid-sized tech company. You plug the company URL into Hunter.io, and it returns a list of verified email addresses with confidence scores. Each match shows how likely it is that the email is accurate.

Hunter.io also offers a domain search feature, which is useful when you want to understand the email format a company uses before reaching out to individual contacts. For entrepreneurs doing targeted B2B outreach, this is incredibly valuable.

**Best for:** Finding specific contact information for research and outreach.

## What Is Brevo and What Does It Do?

Brevo (formerly Sendinblue) is an all-in-one marketing automation platform. It combines email marketing, SMS marketing, CRM, and marketing automation—all in one dashboard.

While Hunter.io is specialized in finding emails, Brevo is designed to help you manage every step of your communication strategy. You can build email campaigns, segment your audience, automate follow-ups, and track results. Brevo also includes a transactional email service, so you can send automated notifications alongside your marketing campaigns.

**Best for:** Managing your entire customer communication strategy after you've found your contacts.

## Key Differences: Hunter.io vs Brevo

| Feature | Hunter.io | Brevo |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| **Primary Function** | Email finding and verification | Email marketing platform |
| **Email Discovery** | Robust, specialized | Limited (requires manual import) |
| **Email Campaigns** | No | Yes, with automation |
| **CRM Features** | No | Yes |
| **Pricing Model** | Pay-per-credit system | Monthly subscription |
| **Learning Curve** | Very simple | Moderate |
| **Best Use Case** | Research and list building | Campaign management and automation |

## How These Tools Work Together

Here's the reality: most successful entrepreneurs use both tools, but at different stages.

**Step 1: Find your contacts.** Use Hunter.io to research companies and build a list of email addresses. This is where you invest time in finding the right people.

**Step 2: Import and nurture.** Take those email addresses and import them into Brevo. Now you can segment them, create personalized email sequences, and track opens and clicks.

**Step 3: Automate your follow-up.** Set up automated campaigns in Brevo that nurture leads while you focus on delivering services to existing clients.

This workflow makes sense because Hunter.io is laser-focused on one task (finding emails), while Brevo handles everything after that.

## Practical Tips for Using These Tools Effectively

**When to prioritize Hunter.io:** If you're doing highly targeted B2B outreach to specific companies, invest in Hunter.io first. The time you save researching contacts is worth the cost, especially if your conversion rate is high.

**When to prioritize Brevo:** If you already have a list of contacts or you're building an audience through other channels (like LinkedIn or your website), Brevo's email marketing features will pay for themselves through better segmentation and higher engagement.

**Hybrid approach:** Many consultants use Hunter.io for initial research, then filter results through LinkedIn to verify they've found the right person, then import to Brevo for campaigns.

## Which Tool Should You Choose?

The answer depends on your stage and needs:

**Choose Hunter.io if:**
- You're doing cold email outreach to specific companies
- You need to verify email addresses before reaching out
- Your main challenge is finding contact information
- You have a smaller budget and want to spend only on what you need

**Choose Brevo if:**
- You already have a contact list (or are building one organically)
- You want to automate your email sequences and nurture leads
- You need CRM features to track customer relationships
- You send regular marketing campaigns or newsletters

**Choose both if:**
- You're scaling your outreach and need a systematic process
- You want to combine targeted prospecting with automated nurturing
- Your business model relies on consistent email communication

## Final Recommendation

For most coaches, consultants, and entrepreneurs, **start with Brevo**. You can build your contact list manually or through other channels, and Brevo's email marketing and automation features will help you convert them into clients.

Only add Hunter.io once you're ready to scale cold outreach as a dedicated growth channel. If cold email becomes a core part of your strategy, Hunter.io's email finding capability will become invaluable.

The best tool is the one that solves your immediate problem. Focus on finding your first clients first—then optimize your systems for scale.
