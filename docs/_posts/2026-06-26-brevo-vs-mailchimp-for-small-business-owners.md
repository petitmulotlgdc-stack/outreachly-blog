# [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/?via=outreachly) vs [Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com/?via=outreachly) for Small Business Owners: Which Email Marketing Platform Wins?

If you're running a coaching practice, consulting business, or any small enterprise, you know that email is still the highest ROI marketing channel. But choosing the right email marketing platform can make the difference between thriving campaigns and frustrating limitations.

Two platforms dominate the small business space: Brevo and Mailchimp. Both promise to help you build relationships with your audience, but they work quite differently. Let me break down which one is actually better for your business.

## Understanding Your Email Marketing Needs

Before diving into features, let's be honest about what small business owners actually need. You need a platform that's easy to use, doesn't require a marketing degree, and won't drain your budget. You probably want to segment your audience, send automated sequences, and track what's working.

The challenge is that not every tool excels at everything. Some are generous with free tiers but charge premium prices as you grow. Others have powerful automation but confusing interfaces.

## Feature Comparison: Brevo vs Mailchimp

| Feature | Brevo | Mailchimp |
|---------|-------|----------|
| **Free Plan Limit** | Unlimited contacts, 300 emails/day | Up to 500 contacts, unlimited emails |
| **Automation** | Full automation included on free plan | Limited on free plan (basic workflows) |
| **SMS Marketing** | Included (SMS campaigns + SMS automation) | Add-on feature (paid) |
| **CRM Features** | Built-in CRM included | Basic contact management |
| **Support** | 24/7 live chat support | Email support (limited on free) |
| **Ease of Use** | Intuitive interface, good for beginners | Learning curve, more features = complexity |
| **Pricing Model** | Pay per email volume | Pay per contact |

## Brevo: The All-in-One Approach

Brevo takes a refreshing approach to small business email marketing. Their free plan is genuinely generous—you get unlimited contacts and 300 emails per day, which is substantial for most coaches and consultants.

What makes Brevo stand out is what's included. Their free plan contains full automation capabilities, meaning you can build welcome sequences, nurture funnels, and trigger-based campaigns without paying a dime. This is huge for service-based businesses that rely on automating client onboarding.

The built-in CRM is another game-changer. You can track client interactions, manage conversations, and organize your pipeline—all without a separate tool. If you're managing multiple client relationships, this integration saves time and reduces tool switching.

Brevo also includes SMS marketing on all plans. For coaches and consultants who want to reach clients through multiple channels, this is valuable without the typical add-on cost.

The trade-off? Brevo's interface takes some getting used to if you're completely new to email marketing, though it's generally praised as clean and modern.

## Mailchimp: The Familiar Choice

Mailchimp has been the go-to email platform for over a decade, and there's good reason. The interface feels intuitive to most people, the templates are professionally designed, and the learning curve is gentler.

For pure email marketing, Mailchimp excels. Their automation features are solid, though on the free plan they're more limited compared to Brevo. You can set up basic workflows and automation sequences, but advanced segmentation and complex automations require paid upgrades.

Mailchimp's free plan supports up to 500 contacts with unlimited emails—excellent if you have a smaller list. However, once you exceed this, you move to contact-based pricing, which can escalate quickly as your list grows.

One thing to consider: Mailchimp is moving increasingly toward being a complete marketing platform. They've added landing pages, ads, e-commerce features, and more. This is great if you want everything in one place, but it also means more complexity if you only need email.

## Which Platform Is Right for You?

**Choose Brevo if you:**
- Want unlimited contacts on a free plan
- Need SMS marketing capabilities
- Run email sequences and nurture campaigns regularly
- Want a built-in CRM for client management
- Prefer transparent, straightforward pricing as you scale

**Choose Mailchimp if you:**
- Are just starting and have fewer than 500 contacts
- Value simplicity and an established platform
- Already use their other tools (ads, landing pages)
- Prefer a platform with extensive community resources and tutorials

## The Bottom Line

For most coaches, consultants, and small business owners, **Brevo edges out as the better choice** in 2024. The unlimited contacts on the free plan, included automation, built-in CRM, and SMS capabilities offer more value without paying premium prices.

However, if you're just launching and want the simplest possible interface, Mailchimp remains solid. The key is testing both with your actual contact list and workflow needs.

Start with Brevo's free plan if you want automation and SMS included. You can always migrate to Mailchimp later if needed—though honestly, most users find Brevo grows with them without hitting the cost ceiling that catches people off-guard with Mailchimp.

Your email list is your most valuable asset. Choose the platform that lets you nurture relationships without technical friction.
