# [Monday.com](https://monday.com/?via=outreachly) vs [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/?via=outreachly) for Freelancers: Which Project Management Platform Wins?

As a freelancer or small business consultant, managing client projects, deadlines, and communication can feel overwhelming. You're juggling multiple tasks—from tracking deliverables to following up on invoices—and you need a system that keeps everything organized without eating up your productivity time.

Two platforms consistently come up when freelancers search for solutions: Monday.com and HubSpot. But which one actually delivers what you need? Let's break down how these tools compare so you can make an informed decision for your business.

## What Makes Monday.com Stand Out for Freelancers

Monday.com is built from the ground up as a project management platform. It's visual, intuitive, and designed specifically for teams managing multiple projects simultaneously.

**Key strengths for freelancers:**

The platform excels at task organization. You can create projects, break them into subtasks, set dependencies, and assign deadlines. The visual board layout—similar to Kanban—makes it easy to see at a glance where each project stands. Whether you're managing client work, internal processes, or team collaboration, Monday.com keeps everything transparent.

Monday.com also offers strong automation. You can automate repetitive tasks like status updates, notifications, and handoffs between team members. For freelancers scaling beyond solo work, this saves countless hours.

The mobile app is robust, meaning you can manage projects on the go—a critical feature if you're meeting clients or working remotely.

## HubSpot's Unique Advantage: The CRM-Focused Approach

HubSpot positions itself differently. While it includes project management features, its core strength is customer relationship management (CRM). For consultants and coaches who need to nurture client relationships alongside project delivery, this distinction matters.

**Key strengths for service-based professionals:**

HubSpot's CRM is powerful. You can track every interaction with a client—emails, calls, proposals, contracts. This creates a complete history that helps you understand your client relationships better and identify upselling opportunities.

The free tier is genuinely generous. You get contact management, basic email tracking, and deal pipelines without paying anything. This makes HubSpot an excellent starting point if you're a solo practitioner watching your budget carefully.

HubSpot's sales pipeline features are more sophisticated than Monday.com's. You can create custom deal stages, track win/loss reasons, and forecast revenue—essential for consultants managing multiple client contracts.

## Pricing and Scalability Considerations

Monday.com's pricing starts at $9 per seat per month (annual billing), making it relatively affordable for small teams. However, if you're a solo freelancer, you're still paying for a seat-based model.

HubSpot's free CRM tier is genuinely useful for freelancers starting out. Paid plans begin at $50/month for the Sales Hub, increasing as you add features. The good news? You only pay once, not per team member.

For solo freelancers on a tight budget, HubSpot's free tier wins. For growing teams needing robust project management, Monday.com's transparent pricing becomes more attractive.

## Which Type of Business Needs Which Platform?

**Choose Monday.com if you:**
- Manage multiple concurrent projects with complex dependencies
- Have a small team that needs clear visibility across all work
- Value visual workflow management and ease of use
- Need strong automation for repetitive processes
- Want a dedicated project management solution without CRM complexity

**Choose HubSpot if you:**
- Need sophisticated client relationship tracking
- Want to combine CRM and project management in one platform
- Value email integration and communication history
- Benefit from sales pipeline and deal forecasting
- Want to start with a generous free tier

## Final Recommendation

For most freelancers and small business coaches, **HubSpot edges ahead**—specifically because of its free CRM tier. If you're solo or just starting, HubSpot's free plan handles your core needs without requiring payment. Its email integration and contact management are superior, which matters when your business depends on strong client relationships.

However, if you have team members, manage 5+ projects simultaneously, or need advanced task automation, **Monday.com is the better choice**. Its project-centric design and automation capabilities justify the investment.

The reality? Many successful consultants use both in tandem—HubSpot for client relationships and Monday.com for detailed project delivery tracking. But if you must choose one, start with HubSpot's free tier, see if you outgrow it, then add Monday.com for project management as you scale.

Your business will tell you which platform you need. Start with the free option, test it with real work, and upgrade when the limitations become painful.
