# [Monday.com](https://monday.com/?via=outreachly) vs [Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com/?via=outreachly): Which Tool Should Small Business Owners Choose?

As a coach, consultant, or small business owner, you're juggling countless tasks—client management, marketing campaigns, project deadlines, and team coordination. Two tools keep popping up in your search results: Monday.com and Mailchimp. But here's the problem: they solve different problems, and choosing the wrong one could waste your time and money.

In this guide, I'll break down exactly what each platform does, how they compare, and which one (or both) might be right for your business.

## What Each Tool Actually Does

Before we compare, let's clarify what you're actually getting with each platform.

**Monday.com** is a work operating system. Think of it as your command center for project management, task tracking, and team collaboration. It helps you visualize workflows, manage client projects, track deadlines, and keep everyone on the same page.

**Mailchimp** is an email marketing platform. It's designed for building email lists, creating campaigns, automating email sequences, and tracking subscriber engagement. It's your tool for reaching customers via their inbox.

These platforms solve fundamentally different problems. It's not a matter of choosing one over the other—it's understanding whether you need one, both, or neither.

## When You Need Monday.com

Monday.com shines for coaches and consultants managing client projects with moving parts. Here's when it's worth the investment:

**You have multiple active projects.** If you're juggling three client retainers, a product launch, and your own business operations simultaneously, Monday.com creates visibility. You can see what's due, who's responsible, and what's blocked.

**You work with a team.** Whether it's virtual assistants, contractors, or employees, Monday.com keeps everyone aligned. Task assignments, status updates, and deadline reminders reduce miscommunication and follow-up emails.

**You need workflow automation.** The platform allows you to create automated workflows that trigger actions based on status changes. For example: when a client contract is signed, it automatically creates a project timeline and sends a welcome email.

**Real example:** A consultant running multiple client engagements might use Monday.com to track onboarding, deliverables, and client feedback—all in one visual workspace.

## When You Need Mailchimp

Mailchimp is essential if you're building a business that relies on email marketing and audience engagement.

**You're building an email list.** Whether you're collecting leads from a website, webinar, or free resource, Mailchimp makes it easy to capture and organize subscribers. Forms can be embedded on your site in minutes.

**You send regular marketing campaigns.** If you're sharing content, promotions, or updates with your audience, Mailchimp's drag-and-drop editor makes designing emails fast—no coding skills required.

**You want automation sequences.** Welcome series, abandoned cart emails, or nurture sequences can run on autopilot. Set them once, and they work while you sleep.

**You need segmentation.** Mailchimp lets you segment subscribers by behavior, interests, or demographics. This means coaches can send different messages to prospects versus existing clients automatically.

**Real example:** A coach building their personal brand might use Mailchimp to collect newsletter subscribers, send weekly tips, and nurture leads toward booking calls.

## Can You Use Both Together?

Absolutely. In fact, many small business owners benefit from combining both tools.

Monday.com can integrate with Mailchimp, allowing you to:
- Track email campaign performance within your project dashboard
- Automate task creation when campaigns are scheduled
- Connect subscriber metrics to client outcomes

The workflow might look like this: You schedule an email campaign in Mailchimp, it triggers a task in Monday.com to track results, and your team gets a notification to follow up on engagement metrics.

## Pricing Consideration

**Monday.com** starts at $9/month for one user but scales quickly as you add team members. Most coaches find themselves investing $30-60/month once they add automations and multiple users.

**Mailchimp** offers a genuinely free plan for up to 500 contacts, making it cost-free for early-stage businesses. Paid plans start at $20/month for additional features.

For bootstrapped consultants, Mailchimp's free option is hard to beat if email marketing is your main need.

## The Bottom Line: Which Should You Choose?

Here's the honest answer: **Choose based on your biggest bottleneck.**

- **Struggling to manage client projects and deadlines?** → Monday.com
- **Building an email list and want to send regular campaigns?** → Mailchimp
- **Growing beyond one person and need both visibility and audience?** → Both tools complement each other

For most coaches and consultants, I recommend starting with Mailchimp (free tier) to validate that email marketing works for your business. Once your list hits 500-1000 subscribers and you're managing multiple client projects, add Monday.com.

The best tool isn't about features—it's about solving your specific problem today, at a price you can afford, without overwhelming your workflow.
