## What Is Apollo and How Does It Work?

Apollo is a B2B sales intelligence and outreach platform designed to help professionals find, manage, and engage with prospects. Think of it as a combination sales database and automation tool that makes prospecting less painful.

Here's what Apollo does at its core:

**Lead Database and Search**: Apollo maintains a database of over 275 million business contacts and 70+ million companies. You can search by industry, company size, location, job title, technology stack, and dozens of other criteria. This beats manually Googling prospects or buying expensive lead lists.

**Email Finding**: Beyond searching existing records, Apollo uses algorithms to find email addresses for professionals who might not have publicly listed contact information. The platform claims a 98% email accuracy rate, which is impressive for cold outreach.

**Outreach and Automation**: Apollo integrates with your email to automate personalized outreach sequences. You can set up campaigns that send emails based on trigger events, engagement metrics, and custom conditions. It's not true AI-powered personalization, but it's more sophisticated than basic mail merges.

**CRM Features**: Apollo includes a lightweight CRM where you can manage contacts, track deal stages, and monitor email interactions. It's integrated right into the platform, so you're not juggling multiple tools.

**Analytics and Reporting**: You get insights into campaign performance, response rates, email open tracking, and engagement metrics that help you refine your outreach strategy.

For consultants specifically, this means you can prospect more efficiently, track which outreach actually works, and maintain organized client pipelines without Excel spreadsheets.

## Key Features That Matter for Consultants

Let's talk about which Apollo features actually move the needle for consulting businesses.

### Lead Intelligence and Filtering

Apollo's search functionality is genuinely powerful. You can filter prospects by specific characteristics that matter for your niche. If you're a marketing consultant targeting mid-market SaaS companies, you can search for "VP of Marketing" at companies with 50-200 employees that use specific marketing tools.

This beats generic lead lists because you're finding genuinely relevant prospects instead of paying for a list where 60% isn't a fit.

For consultants doing outreach, knowing that your message hit the inbox (and understanding engagement) helps you refine messaging before you make calls.

## Pricing: What You'll Actually Pay

This is where many Apollo reviews get vague. Let's be specific.

Apollo offers several tiers:

**Basic Plan ($49/month billed annually)**: Comes with 500 credits monthly (each outreach typically uses 1 credit), access to the database, and manual email finding. Good for side hustles or light prospecting.

**Professional Plan ($99/month billed annually)**: Includes 1,500 credits, unlimited email finding, basic automation, and CRM. This is the sweet spot for most consultants.

**Organization Plan ($199/month billed annually)**: For teams with 3+ members. Includes higher credit allowance, team management features, and advanced workflows.

**Custom Plans**: For enterprise needs with specific requirements.

**The real cost consideration**: Credits refresh monthly, but they're only valuable if you use them. If you're not consistently prospecting, you're paying for unused capacity. However, you can pause your account whenever needed, so you're not locked into dead months.

For a solo consultant sending 30-50 personalized outreach emails weekly, the Professional plan covers your needs. At $99/month ($1,188 annually), this breaks even if it generates even one consulting engagement above that amount.

## Implementation Tips: Getting the Most from Apollo

If you decide to go with Apollo, here's how consultants actually get results:

### 1. Build Targeted Lists First
Don't just search broadly and email everyone. Create specific target lists based on company size, industry, and role. This improves response rates significantly.

### 2. Personalize Beyond Auto-Personalization
Apollo's templates include merge tags for names and companies. Use them, but also customize the opening line of each email to reference something specific about the prospect. "Hi [First Name]" converts worse than "Thanks for writing about [specific achievement] in [publication]."

### 3. Start with Smaller Sequences
Send 30-50 emails to test your messaging before scaling. Track response rates by subject line and opening. What works for SaaS might flop for manufacturing consulting.

### 4. Use the Analytics to Iterate
Apollo tracks opens, clicks, and responses. Check these weekly. If your open rate is below 25%, your subject lines need work. If opens are good but clicks are low, your email body isn't compelling.

### 5. Connect to Your CRM
Whether you use HubSpot, Pipedrive, or another system, sync Apollo contacts there. Apollo's CRM is good, but your primary CRM should be your single source of truth for client relationships.

### 6. Combine with Phone and LinkedIn
Email is part of your strategy, not the whole thing. Use Apollo to find prospects, reach out via email, and if there's no response, connect on LinkedIn or call. Multi-channel outreach significantly improves results.

## Common Questions About Apollo for Consultants

**Q: Will Apollo emails get flagged as spam?**
A: Not typically. Apollo follows email authentication best practices and doesn't use sketchy tactics. Deliverability is strong if you use a professional domain and follow basic email etiquette.

**Q: How long does it take to see results?**
A: Seriously, 2-3 weeks. You need time to send emails, let them sit in prospects' inboxes, and get responses. Building a pipeline takes 4-6 weeks minimum.

**Q: Can I use Apollo if I already have leads?**
A: Absolutely. Use Apollo to find new prospects and manage current conversations in the CRM. It's not an all-or-nothing tool.

**Q: Is the 98% email accuracy real?**
A: It's honestly pretty good. You'll get occasional bad addresses, but the verification system minimizes deliverability issues. The real limitation is that you'll find emails for maybe 70-80% of people you search for—the other 20% don't have publicly available addresses.

**Q: Can I automate follow-ups?**
A: Yes. Apollo's sequences can auto-send follow-up emails based on specific conditions (no reply, email opened, clicked link, etc.). Be careful not to over-automate—too many sequences feels pushy.
