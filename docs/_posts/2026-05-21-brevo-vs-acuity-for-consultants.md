# [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/?via=outreachly) vs Acuity for Consultants: Which Platform Should You Choose?

Finding the right email marketing and scheduling tool can feel overwhelming when you're running a consulting business. You're juggling client communications, appointment management, and marketing campaigns—all while trying to focus on actually delivering results for your clients.

The question many consultants face is simple: should you invest in Brevo or Acuity Scheduling? Both platforms promise to streamline your business operations, but they approach the problem differently. Let's dig into what each offers and help you make an informed decision.

## Understanding Your Consultant's Needs

Before comparing these platforms, it's important to recognize what consultants actually need. You typically require:

- **Email marketing capabilities** to nurture leads and stay in touch with clients
- **Appointment scheduling** to eliminate back-and-forth calendar exchanges
- **Automation** to save time on repetitive tasks
- **Client management** features to organize information
- **Affordable pricing** that scales with your growing business

Both Brevo and Acuity address these needs, but with different strengths. Understanding where each excels helps you choose the right fit for your consulting practice.

## Brevo: The Email Marketing Powerhouse

Brevo is primarily an email marketing and CRM platform that has evolved into a comprehensive business tool. It's particularly strong for consultants who want powerful email capabilities without breaking the bank.

**Key strengths for consultants:**

Brevo offers unlimited contacts on all plans, which means your growing client list won't increase your monthly costs. This is a significant advantage when you're building relationships with multiple clients over time.

The platform includes robust automation features—you can set up email sequences triggered by user behavior, ensuring your follow-ups happen automatically. For consultants, this means nurturing leads while you're focused on current client work.

Brevo's pricing is exceptionally competitive. The free plan includes email marketing for up to 300 contacts, making it perfect for new consultants just starting out. Paid plans start at just a few dollars per month for larger lists.

The SMS marketing feature is an underrated advantage. You can reach clients through text messages for appointment reminders or quick updates, improving show-up rates significantly.

## Acuity Scheduling: The Appointment Specialist

Acuity Scheduling, now part of Squarespace, focuses primarily on appointment booking, scheduling, and client management. It's built specifically for service-based professionals who need sophisticated scheduling capabilities.

**Key strengths for consultants:**

Acuity excels at eliminating scheduling friction. Clients can book appointments directly into your calendar, with automatic confirmation emails and reminders. This saves hours of back-and-forth communication every month.

The platform includes built-in payment processing, allowing you to collect deposits or full fees when clients book. This is particularly valuable for consultants who want to ensure commitment from potential clients.

Custom forms allow you to gather specific information from clients before appointments. You might ask about their main challenges, current situation, or goals—ensuring you're prepared before every call.

Acuity's customer support is notably strong, and the interface is intuitive even for less tech-savvy users. The integration with Squarespace makes it particularly valuable if you already use their website builder.

## Which Platform Should You Choose?

**Choose Brevo if:**
- Email marketing is central to your business model
- You want to nurture large email lists affordably
- You need advanced automation capabilities
- You're budget-conscious and want unlimited contact growth
- SMS outreach is important for your consulting practice

**Choose Acuity Scheduling if:**
- Appointment booking is your primary pain point
- You need integrated payment processing
- You want clients to self-serve on scheduling
- You prefer a simpler, more intuitive interface
- You're already in the Squarespace ecosystem

## The Hybrid Approach

Many successful consultants actually use both tools together. Brevo handles email marketing and client nurturing, while Acuity manages scheduling and appointments. This combination gives you the best of both worlds—powerful email marketing alongside seamless scheduling.

If you're just starting out, Brevo's free plan is hard to beat. You get professional email marketing without paying anything. As your business grows and scheduling becomes more complex, adding Acuity makes sense.

## Final Recommendation

For most consultants, **Brevo is the better starting choice**. Its affordability, unlimited contacts, and powerful automation make it ideal for building and nurturing your client base. The free plan lets you test it without risk.

However, if appointment scheduling is causing you significant friction and you need integrated payments, Acuity Scheduling is worth the investment. The key is choosing the tool that solves your biggest business problem right now, then expanding from there.

Start with whichever addresses your most pressing need today. You can always integrate additional tools as your consulting practice scales.
