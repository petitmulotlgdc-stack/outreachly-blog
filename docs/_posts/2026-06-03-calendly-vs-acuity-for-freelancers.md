# [Calendly](https://calendly.com/?via=outreachly) vs Acuity for Freelancers: Which Scheduling Tool Fits Your Business?

Scheduling client meetings shouldn't consume your valuable time. Yet many freelancers, coaches, and consultants still juggle email chains, back-and-forth messages, and manual calendar updates. The right scheduling tool can eliminate this friction and let you focus on what you do best—delivering results for your clients.

If you're weighing Calendly versus Acuity, you're looking at two popular scheduling platforms. But they serve different needs, and choosing the wrong one can leave you frustrated or paying for features you don't use. Let's break down how these tools compare so you can make an informed decision.

## Core Functionality: Scheduling Made Simple

Both Calendly and Acuity handle the fundamentals of appointment scheduling—letting clients book time on your calendar without back-and-forth emails. However, their approach differs significantly.

Calendly focuses on simplicity. You set your availability, share a link, and clients pick a time. It's straightforward and requires minimal setup. The platform automatically handles timezone conversions, sends reminders, and syncs with your calendar to prevent double-bookings.

Acuity Scheduling goes deeper. Beyond basic scheduling, it includes client management, payment processing, intake forms, and workflows. If you need a more comprehensive business management system wrapped into your scheduling tool, Acuity offers substantially more out of the box.

For freelancers just starting out or those who want a lightweight solution, Calendly's simplicity is appealing. For established consultants managing multiple service types and client touchpoints, Acuity's integrated features save you from juggling separate tools.

## Pricing: Value for Your Budget

Budget matters when you're running a lean operation.

Calendly's free plan is genuinely useful—it includes basic scheduling, one calendar, and standard integrations. Their paid plans ($12-20/month) unlock multiple calendars, advanced automations, and priority support.

Acuity's free plan is more limited but still functional. Their paid plans start at $15/month and scale up based on features. Acuity's pricing can climb higher if you want all features, but many freelancers find value at the mid-tier levels ($25-50/month).

If budget is tight, Calendly's free tier gives you more breathing room. If you're already investing in your business, Acuity's integrated features might justify the extra cost by reducing your need for additional tools.

## Integrations: Playing Well With Your Stack

Today's freelancers rely on multiple tools—email platforms, CRM systems, payment processors, and communication apps.

Calendly integrates with 100+ popular tools including Zapier, Slack, Google Workspace, Microsoft Teams, [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/?via=outreachly), and Stripe. The integration ecosystem is mature and reliable.

Acuity also offers solid integrations, covering similar ground. However, Calendly's broader integration library gives it an edge if you're using niche tools or building a custom workflow through Zapier.

Both platforms sync with your calendar (Google Calendar, Outlook, iCloud) to prevent scheduling conflicts, which is essential functionality neither compromises on.

## Customization and Branding

If your client experience matters (and it should), consider how each platform lets you brand the scheduling experience.

Calendly allows you to customize colors, add your logo, and personalize your scheduling page. It feels like part of your brand, not a third-party tool.

Acuity offers deeper customization. You can build more complex, branded scheduling pages and customize the entire booking flow. This is particularly valuable if you're embedding scheduling on your website or want a cohesive brand experience.

For coaches and consultants focused on premium positioning, Acuity's customization options feel more polished and professional.

## Real-World Scenario: Which Tool Wins?

Consider your typical workflow:

**Choose Calendly if:** You're a freelancer offering one or two service types, want maximum simplicity, need a free tier that actually works, and prefer lightweight tools that integrate with your existing stack.

**Choose Acuity if:** You're a consultant managing multiple service types, need integrated payments, want to collect detailed intake information, build client relationships through the platform, or require more sophisticated automation workflows.

## Final Recommendation

**For most freelancers and emerging coaches, Calendly is the smarter choice.** Its free plan is genuinely useful, the interface is intuitive, and it focuses on doing one thing exceptionally well. The integration ecosystem is stronger, and you'll likely grow into its paid features gradually.

**Choose Acuity if you're ready to consolidate tools and want a platform that handles scheduling plus client management and payments.** It's ideal for established consultants managing complex client journeys.

Start with Calendly, and upgrade to Acuity when you outgrow its capabilities—that's the path most successful freelancers take.
