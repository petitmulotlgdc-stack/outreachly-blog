# [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/?via=outreachly) vs [Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com/?via=outreachly) for Consultants: Which Email Platform Wins?

Your email list is your most valuable asset as a consultant. But choosing the right email marketing platform can make the difference between a thriving communication channel and one that drains your budget and time.

If you're torn between Brevo and Mailchimp, you're looking at two of the market's most popular options. The question isn't which is "better"—it's which fits your consulting business better. Let's break down what each platform offers and help you make an informed decision.

## Key Differences at a Glance

| Feature | Brevo | Mailchimp |
|---------|-------|----------|
| **Free Plan** | Unlimited contacts, 300 emails/day | Up to 500 contacts, unlimited emails |
| **Starting Price** | €20/month (Essential) | Free tier, paid from $20/month |
| **SMS Marketing** | Yes (included) | Add-on required |
| **Automation** | Advanced workflows | Good, but less robust |
| **Landing Pages** | Yes (included) | Available with plan |
| **CRM Integration** | Built-in CRM included | Limited native CRM |
| **Support** | Email, chat, phone | Email, chat (limited on free) |

## Email Features: What Matters for Consultants

Both platforms excel at the fundamentals—building responsive emails, managing lists, and tracking performance. However, they diverge in what's included.

**Brevo** includes SMS marketing in most plans, which is a game-changer for consultants who want to reach clients through multiple channels. You can send appointment reminders, exclusive offers, or urgent updates via text without paying extra.

**Mailchimp** keeps SMS separate, requiring an additional subscription. For consultants managing multiple client touchpoints, this adds complexity and cost.

When it comes to email templates, both offer professionally designed options. Mailchimp's templates tend to be more polished out of the box, while Brevo's are functional and customizable. Neither will slow you down—both take roughly the same time to create and send campaigns.

## Automation and Workflows

Automation is essential for consultants juggling multiple clients and prospects. This is where the platforms truly differentiate.

**Brevo's automation** is more sophisticated. You can build complex workflows with multiple triggers, conditional branching, and integrations with other tools. If you want to nurture different client segments differently—say, prospects versus existing clients—Brevo handles this elegantly.

**Mailchimp's automation** is solid but simpler. It works well for straightforward sequences like welcome series or cart abandonment emails. For consultants managing sophisticated nurture campaigns, it might feel limiting.

If you're running a scaling consulting practice with multiple service tiers or client journeys, Brevo's automation capabilities give you more room to grow without switching platforms later.

## Pricing That Actually Makes Sense

Let's be honest: pricing confusion is real with email platforms.

**Mailchimp's free plan** is genuinely free and handles up to 500 contacts. This works if you're just starting out. However, as soon as you want better features or more contacts, prices jump. Their paid plans start at $20/month but scale quickly based on contact count.

**Brevo's free plan** is unusual—you get unlimited contacts but can send only 300 emails per day. This is actually generous for consultants with smaller lists who need to send occasional campaigns. The paid plans start at €20/month (roughly $22 USD) with more sending capacity.

For consultants managing 1,000-5,000 contacts, Brevo typically costs less because you're not charged per contact. Mailchimp's per-contact pricing means your bill grows with your list size, which can sting as your business scales.

## CRM and Contact Management

Consultants work closely with clients, and having contact history matters.

**Brevo includes a built-in CRM** at no extra cost. You can track conversations, deals, and interactions within the platform. For solo consultants or small teams, this eliminates the need to pay for separate CRM software.

**Mailchimp's CRM features are limited**. You can add tags and notes, but it's not a true CRM experience. If you want real CRM functionality, you'll need to integrate a separate tool—and manage that integration.

This is a significant advantage for consultants who want everything under one roof without paying extra.

## Support and Learning Resources

When you're stuck, responsive support matters.

**Brevo** offers email, live chat, and phone support across all paid plans. They're known for helpful, responsive support teams.

**Mailchimp** provides email and limited chat support. On the free plan, you're largely on your own. They have extensive knowledge bases, but if you need hands-on help, you might wait longer.

## Final Recommendation

**Choose Brevo if:**
- You want SMS marketing included
- You're building sophisticated automation sequences
- You need a CRM without extra cost
- You prefer all-in-one functionality
- You want to avoid per-contact pricing as you scale

**Choose Mailchimp if:**
- You're a complete beginner with a tiny budget
- You prefer a simpler, more straightforward platform
- You like their interface and template designs
- You have a small, static contact list

For most consultants, **Brevo edges ahead**. The included CRM, SMS marketing, and transparent pricing structure make it the smarter long-term investment. As your consulting practice grows, you won't outgrow the platform or face unexpected cost increases based on list size.

Start with a free account on either platform and test-drive it for a week. Your gut feeling after real usage often beats any feature list comparison.
