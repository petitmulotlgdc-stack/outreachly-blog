# [Notion](https://notion.so/?via=outreachly) vs Acuity for Entrepreneurs: Which Tool Fits Your Business Best?

Managing your business as an entrepreneur means juggling a hundred different tasks. Between client scheduling, project management, invoicing, and keeping track of your pipeline, it's easy to feel overwhelmed. Two popular platforms promise to simplify your workflow: Notion and Acuity Scheduling. But which one is actually right for your coaching, consulting, or service-based business?

Let's break down what each tool does and help you make an informed decision.

## What Does Each Tool Do?

**Notion** is a versatile all-in-one workspace that combines databases, documents, wikis, and project management. Think of it as a blank canvas where you can build almost anything—from client CRM systems to content calendars to knowledge bases.

**Acuity Scheduling** is a dedicated appointment scheduling and client management platform. It's designed specifically for service-based businesses that need to manage client bookings, payments, and follow-ups.

The key difference? Notion is a generalist tool, while Acuity is a specialist.

## Scheduling and Appointment Management

When it comes to handling client bookings, Acuity Scheduling is purpose-built for this task.

Acuity lets clients book directly from your website with automated confirmations, reminders, and follow-up emails. You can set availability, buffer times between appointments, and even require deposits or full payment upfront. The calendar integrates seamlessly with your business operations.

Notion can display a calendar view, and you can build a booking system using databases and integrations, but it requires more setup work. You'd likely need to integrate Notion with another tool like Zapier to create a fully functional booking system.

**Winner for scheduling:** Acuity Scheduling wins here for most entrepreneurs. It's faster to implement and requires zero technical setup.

## Client Management and CRM Features

Notion excels as a CRM platform because of its flexibility. You can create client databases with custom fields, track project progress, store notes, and build entire workflows tailored to your business. Many coaches and consultants use Notion as their single source of truth for all client information.

Acuity also includes client management features. You get contact information, appointment history, and can track client communication. However, it's more basic than what you can build in Notion and is tightly integrated with scheduling functionality.

If you want a powerful, customizable CRM without additional costs, Notion is the better choice. If you want something quick and scheduling-focused, Acuity's CRM capabilities are sufficient.

## Pricing and Value for Money

**Notion** offers excellent value. The free version is surprisingly robust and handles most small business needs. The paid plan is just $10-20 per month, making it extremely affordable.

**Acuity Scheduling** pricing depends on your needs. The Standard plan starts at $16/month, Professional at $36/month, and Premier at $76/month. These higher tiers add features like advanced reporting, custom branding, and API access.

For entrepreneurs watching their budget, Notion provides more features for less money. However, Acuity's pricing reflects its specialization and efficiency.

## Integration Capabilities

Notion integrates with numerous tools through Zapier, making it possible to connect your workspace with email, Slack, invoicing software, and more.

Acuity has native integrations with email platforms, payment processors (Stripe, PayPal), calendar apps, and marketing tools. It also works with Zapier for additional flexibility.

Both tools play well with others, but Acuity's integrations are more seamlessly connected to scheduling workflows.

## Which Tool Should You Choose?

**Choose Notion if you:**
- Want a single platform to manage clients, projects, and workflows
- Value customization and flexibility
- Have a tight budget
- Don't mind spending time setting up your system
- Need a comprehensive CRM alongside other business functions

**Choose Acuity Scheduling if you:**
- Need reliable client booking with minimal setup time
- Want a dedicated system that "just works"
- Prefer specialist tools over generalist platforms
- Need automated payment collection
- Have regular recurring appointments

## The Bottom Line

For most entrepreneurs, the answer isn't either/or—it's both. Many consultants and coaches use Acuity Scheduling to handle appointments and payments, then use Notion to manage broader business operations, client information, and strategic planning.

However, if you can only choose one tool, Notion offers better overall value and flexibility for entrepreneurs building their business infrastructure. It requires more initial setup but pays dividends as your business grows.

Acuity Scheduling is ideal if appointment management is your primary pain point and you want something that requires zero configuration.

Start with a free trial of both, map out your specific needs, and choose based on which tool aligns with how you actually work.
