# [Calendly](https://calendly.com/?via=outreachly) vs Acuity: Which Scheduling Tool Is Best for Your Coaching Business?

If you're running a coaching, consulting, or service-based business, you've probably felt the pain of coordinating client meetings via email. Back-and-forth messages about availability, timezone confusion, and missed appointments drain your time and frustrate your clients. The solution? A scheduling tool.

Two names dominate the conversation: Calendly and Acuity. But which one actually serves small business owners better? The answer depends on your specific needs, budget, and business model. Let's break down both platforms so you can make an informed decision.

## Understanding the Core Difference

Both Calendly and Acuity solve the same fundamental problem—they eliminate scheduling chaos. However, they approach the solution differently.

Calendly is lightweight and straightforward. It's designed for professionals who primarily need to book one-on-one meetings without the additional business overhead.

Acuity takes a more comprehensive approach. It's a full-featured scheduling and client management platform built specifically for service-based businesses. Beyond scheduling, Acuity handles intake forms, payment processing, automated reminders, and client workflows.

This fundamental difference matters when you're choosing between them.

## Calendly: Best for Simple, Fast Setup

Calendly excels when simplicity is your priority. You can be up and running in under 10 minutes. Connect your calendar, share your link, and start booking clients.

**Best for:**
- Solo coaches and consultants just starting out
- Professionals who need basic scheduling only
- Those who prefer minimal learning curves
- Business owners with tight budgets

**Key advantages:**
- Intuitive interface anyone can understand immediately
- Free tier covers most solo practitioners' needs
- Seamless integrations with popular tools like Zoom, Google Workspace, and Slack
- Reliable payment processing when you upgrade
- Clean, professional booking pages that build client trust

**Limitations:**
- Limited automation compared to competitors
- Payment processing requires a paid plan
- Fewer customization options for client experience
- Can feel basic if you need advanced features later

## Acuity: Best for Growing Service-Based Businesses

Acuity Scheduling (now part of Squarespace) is engineered for professionals who want everything in one place. It's particularly valuable if you're managing multiple service offerings, collecting client information upfront, or processing payments regularly.

**Best for:**
- Established coaches with multiple service packages
- Therapists and wellness professionals managing client workflows
- Consultants who need intake forms and questionnaires
- Businesses handling payment processing frequently
- Entrepreneurs planning to scale

**Key advantages:**
- Integrated payment processing saves you money on credit card fees
- Powerful automation workflows reduce manual admin work
- Custom intake forms gather client information before meetings
- Client portal provides transparency and professionalism
- Advanced reporting helps you understand business patterns
- Built-in templates for various service types

**Limitations:**
- Steeper learning curve than Calendly
- No truly free tier (14-day trial available)
- Can feel over-engineered for simple scheduling needs
- Pricing increases as you add advanced features

## Pricing Reality Check

For solo consultants, Calendly's free plan often suffices. The paid tier ($10-20/month) adds payment processing and advanced features.

Acuity's pricing starts around $19-99/month depending on features, with no free option. However, if you're processing payments frequently, Acuity's integrated payment system (lower fees, no separate tool needed) often pays for itself.

Calculate your actual costs: a consultant processing just three client payments monthly will likely save money with Acuity's integrated processing versus Calendly plus a separate payment processor.

## Making Your Decision: Key Questions

Ask yourself these questions:

1. **Do I process payments regularly?** → Acuity wins (integrated processing)
2. **Do I need intake forms and client questionnaires?** → Acuity wins (built-in)
3. **Am I managing one straightforward service?** → Calendly wins (simpler)
4. **Is my time worth prioritizing ease of setup?** → Calendly wins (faster)
5. **Do I want comprehensive client management?** → Acuity wins (more tools)
6. **Is my budget under $20/month?** → Calendly wins (free option exists)

## The Verdict: Our Recommendation

**Start with Calendly if:** You're a solo practitioner, consultant, or new coach with simple scheduling needs and a limited budget. You want something working today without complications.

**Upgrade to Acuity if:** You're processing client payments regularly, managing multiple services, or building a scalable practice. You're willing to invest time learning the platform for long-term efficiency gains.

Honestly? Many small business owners start with Calendly and graduate to Acuity as their practices grow. There's no shame in that progression. Choose what fits your business stage now, knowing you can evolve when your needs demand it.

The best scheduling tool is the one you'll actually use consistently. Both Calendly and Acuity accomplish that. Your job is matching the right tool to your current business reality.
