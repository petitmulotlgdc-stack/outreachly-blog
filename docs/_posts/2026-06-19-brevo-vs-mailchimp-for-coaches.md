# [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/?via=outreachly) vs [Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com/?via=outreachly) for Coaches: Which Email Marketing Platform Wins?

As a coach or consultant, your email list is one of your most valuable assets. But choosing the right platform to manage it can feel overwhelming. With so many options available, how do you know if Brevo or Mailchimp is the better fit for your coaching business?

Both platforms promise to help you build relationships with clients and grow your revenue through email marketing. Yet they work quite differently, and what works for one coach might not work for another. In this guide, we'll break down the key differences so you can make an informed decision.

## Pricing: Where Your Budget Goes

When you're building a coaching business, every dollar counts. Let's talk about what you'll actually pay.

**Mailchimp** starts free with up to 500 contacts and limited features. Once you exceed that, pricing jumps to around $20-$350+ per month depending on subscriber count and features needed.

**Brevo** offers a more generous free plan: up to 300 emails per day (unlimited contacts) with essential features included. Paid plans start at roughly $20 per month but scale more affordably as your list grows.

For coaches just starting out, Brevo's free plan is significantly more forgiving. You can build a substantial email list before paying anything. This makes it ideal if you're testing your coaching offer or launching for the first time.

## Ease of Use: Getting Up and Running

You don't have time to become an email marketing expert. You have clients to serve.

**Mailchimp** has a straightforward interface that feels intuitive at first glance. Creating a basic email campaign takes minutes. However, more advanced features like automation sequences can feel clunky if you're not technical.

**Brevo** offers a cleaner, more modern interface. The automation builder is drag-and-drop friendly, making it easier to create sequences without technical knowledge. Setting up welcome sequences, nurture campaigns, or client follow-ups requires fewer clicks.

For coaches managing multiple aspects of their business, Brevo's interface saves time. You'll spend less time figuring out the software and more time serving your clients.

## Email Automation: Nurturing Your Leads

As a coach, you probably want to automate your follow-up sequence. This is where these platforms truly differentiate.

**Mailchimp's** automation features exist, but they're basic compared to specialized platforms. Creating complex sequences with conditional logic can require workarounds or integration with other tools.

**Brevo** excels here. It includes advanced automation with if/then logic, behavioral triggers, and multi-step sequences built in. You can create sophisticated workflows that nurture leads based on their actions. For example, you can automatically segment people who click a specific link or open multiple emails.

If you're planning to build a sales funnel or automated nurture sequence, Brevo gives you more power without extra cost.

## Customer Support: Getting Help When You Need It

Nothing's worse than being stuck on a technical issue when you're trying to launch a campaign.

**Mailchimp** offers email and chat support, though response times on the free plan can be slow. Their knowledge base is extensive but sometimes feels overwhelming.

**Brevo** provides 24/7 email support and live chat across all plans. For coaches in different time zones, this matters. You can typically get a response within a few hours, even on the free plan.

## Integration Capabilities

Most coaches use other tools—calendar scheduling, payment processors, CRM software. Both platforms integrate with popular tools, but Brevo has deeper integrations with coaching-specific software like [Calendly](https://calendly.com/?via=outreachly), Stripe, and Zapier, making automation workflows more seamless.

## Which Platform Should You Choose?

**Choose Mailchimp if:**
- You're just getting started with minimal budget
- You prefer simplicity and don't need complex automation
- Your primary goal is basic email newsletters

**Choose Brevo if:**
- You're building automated sales funnels or nurture sequences
- You want unlimited contacts on the free plan
- You need responsive customer support
- You plan to scale your coaching business

## The Bottom Line

For most coaches and consultants, **Brevo is the better choice**. The generous free plan lets you start without financial risk, the automation features help you nurture leads effortlessly, and the customer support is responsive when you need help.

Mailchimp works fine for basic email newsletters, but if you're serious about building a coaching business with automated systems, Brevo's features and pricing align better with your growth trajectory.

Start with Brevo's free plan, build your list, and test your messaging. You can always switch later, but most coaches who try Brevo stick with it—that's the real test of a good platform.
